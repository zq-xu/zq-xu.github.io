# Categorized Basic Maltese Vocabulary

This reference groups basic vocabulary into ten core categories. Relevant forms are included directly in the tables. Maltese has no infinitive exactly equivalent to English `to + verb`; verbs are shown in common citation forms, with person-specific forms in [Personal Pronouns and Verb Conjugation](03-person-conjugation.md).

## Notes

- Adjectives commonly have masculine and feminine forms, such as `tajjeb` and `tajba`.
- `x'` is commonly used for “what,” as in `X'jismek?` (What is your name?); it is often written attached to the following word.
- The verb forms above are not shared by all persons. For example, “I eat” is `niekol`, while “he eats” is `jiekol`; see [Personal Pronouns and Verb Conjugation](03-person-conjugation.md).
