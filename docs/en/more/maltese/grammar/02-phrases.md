## Usage Notes

- `jekk jogħġbok` is commonly used in polite requests; the verb may need to change when addressing more than one person.
- Adjectives may change according to the speaker's or noun's grammatical gender, for example `tajjeb` / `tajba` and `veġetarjan` / `veġetarjana`.
- These are beginner examples. In conversation, choose the form that best fits the region, audience, and context.
