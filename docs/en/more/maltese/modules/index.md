# Maltese Module Analysis

This folder contains page-by-page analyses of the Maltese textbook modules, going page by page with English translations and word notes under the Arabic numerals of the textbook footer (the cover is not numbered).

1. [Module 3: When Things Go Wrong](maltese-module3-analysis.md): covering lost items, being late, accidents, and service complaints.
2. [Module 4: Shopping and Food](maltese-module4-analysis.md): covering the shopping dialogue, fruit and vegetable vocabulary, a recipe, numbers and the dual.
3. [Module 5: Travel](maltese-module5-analysis.md): covering the airport and travel passages line by line, adjective gender and comparison, verb stems and the past tense, oral topics, the hundred-word writing task, the informal letter and the transport matching.
4. [Module 6: Consolidation and Personalisation](maltese-module6-analysis.md): covering the seven oral topics, negation, the definite article, pronouns and possession, colour descriptions, plurals and numerals, a sentence for each month, occupations, and the reading questions and true-or-false corrections on Sara's diary.
