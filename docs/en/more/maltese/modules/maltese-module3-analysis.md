# Module 3: When Things Go Wrong

**Module title: Meta l-affarijiet ma jmorrux kif nixtiequ**  
**English title: When things go wrong**

## Module Title Breakdown

| Maltese | English | Word-by-word breakdown |
|---|---|---|
| Meta l-affarijiet ma jmorrux kif nixtiequ | When things don't go as we wish | **Meta** when<br>**l-affarijiet** things (article l- + affarijiet)<br>**ma jmorrux** they don't go well (ma … -x forms the negative; positive form jmorru, literally "they go")<br>**kif** as, the way<br>**nixtiequ** we wish, we want (first person plural). |

The English title **When things go wrong** is close to "when things go wrong"; the Maltese title reads more literally as "when things don't go as we wish". This module practises expressing yourself when things go wrong, through situations such as losing things, being late, accidents, and service complaints.

## Overall Outline

| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Two everyday dialogues: looking for a lost ring, asking for help when late; comprehension questions |
| 3–5 | Spoken situational expressions, restaurant complaint review, written complaint letter |
| 6–11 | Noun singular and plural; plural formation types; identifying plural nouns in sentences |
| 12–15 | Prepositions of place; describing object positions from pictures and practising noun plurals |

### Reading Notes

- **il-/l-** definite article, similar to English *the*; in context it can be translated "this/that" or left untranslated.
- **fil-** in the (preposition + article, written together).
- **tal-** of the (preposition + article, written together).
- **bil-** with/by the (preposition + article, written together).
- **plural sħiħ** regular/full plural, usually formed by adding an ending.
- **plural miksur** broken plural; the stem changes internally and cannot be derived by a single uniform rule.

### Rachel's Review

> **Ma nirrakkomandax ir-ristorant "Bella Malta" li jinsab Marsaxlokk minħabba s-servizz ħażin li rċivejna!**
>
> I don't recommend the "Bella Malta" restaurant in Marsaxlokk because of the bad service we received!
>
> - **ma nirrakkomandax** I don't recommend
> - **ir-ristorant** the restaurant
> - **li** that
> - **jinsab** is located
> - **minħabba** because of
> - **is-servizz** the service
> - **ħażin** bad
> - **rċivejna** we received.

> **Meta poġġejna domna nistennew nofs siegħa sakemm ġie l-wejter jagħtina l-menus, u dam tliet kwarti oħra biex jieħu l-ordni.**
>
> After we sat down we waited half an hour before the waiter came to give us the menus, and then he took another three quarters of an hour to take the order.
>
> - **meta** when
> - **poġġejna** we sat down
> - **domna** we took (time)
> - **nistennew** waiting
> - **nofs siegħa** half an hour
> - **sakemm** until
> - **ġie** he came
> - **il-wejter** the waiter
> - **jagħtina** gives us
> - **il-menus** the menus (plural)
> - **u** and
> - **dam** he took
> - **tliet kwarti oħra** another three quarters
> - **biex** to / before
> - **jieħu** he takes
> - **l-ordni** the order.

> **Agħar minn hekk, meta l-ordni waslet kienet ħażina u minfuq ried ir-raġun il-wejter!**
>
> Worse still, when the order arrived it was wrong, and what's more the waiter insisted he was right!
>
> - **agħar** worse
> - **minn hekk** than that
> - **meta** when
> - **waslet** it arrived (feminine, the order)
> - **kienet ħażina** it was wrong
> - **u** and
> - **minfuq** what's more
> - **ried** he insisted/wanted
> - **ir-raġun** being right
> - **il-wejter** the waiter.

### Correspondence with the Page 12 Vocabulary

- **fuq** on: picture 1.
- **taħt** under: picture 7.
- **quddiem** in front of: picture 4.
- **wara** behind: picture 5; the difference from picture 3 is that the apple is partly hidden by the box.
- **bejn** between: picture 6; the plural **il-kaxxi** is used because the reference objects are two boxes.
- **ħdejn / maġenb** next to: picture 3 could also be **It-tuffieħa qiegħda ħdejn il-kaxxa.**, but that doesn't say left or right.
- **fuq in-naħa tal-lemin** on the right side: the more specific expression for picture 3. The whole phrase means "on the right side"; don't translate only the **fuq** in it as "above".
- **fuq in-naħa tax-xellug** on the left side: picture 4 is also somewhat left in the frame, so one could add **It-tuffieħa qiegħda fuq in-naħa tax-xellug tal-kaxxa.**; however, since it covers the front of the box, **quddiem** is the more accurate main relation.
- **fil-kaxxa** in the box: picture 2, and provisionally picture 8; not "on top of the box".
- **madwar** around: picture 9; distinct from **bejn** (between two or more reference objects).

**Still to check:** the box boundaries and the apple overlap in picture 8 are unclear; "inside" is not treated as the only certain answer. Picture 9 uses the collective noun **tuffieħ** rather than the single apple **tuffieħa**, so **qiegħed** is used accordingly.

## Review Points

1. **Asking about and explaining a situation:**  
	*X’ġralek?* (What happened to you?)  
	*Ma nafx* (I don't know)  
	*Skużani* (Sorry / excuse me).

	*Xi ħadd jista’ jgħinni?* (Can someone help me?)  
	*Nixtieq inkellem lill-maniġer* (I'd like to speak to the manager).

	*fuq* on  
	*taħt* under  
	*quddiem* in front of  
	*wara* behind  
	*bejn* between  
	*ħdejn/maġenb* next to.
