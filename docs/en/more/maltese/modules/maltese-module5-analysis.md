## 2 Gender and Comparison of Adjectives

| Original sentence or example | Translation and notes |
|---|---|
| L-aġġettiv jiddeskrivi n-nom u dan ukoll għandu ġens. | The adjective describes the noun, and the noun also has a gender.<br>**L-aġġettiv** the adjective<br>**jiddeskrivi** describes<br>**n-nom** the noun<br>**u dan ukoll** and this too<br>**għandu** has<br>**ġens** grammatical gender. |
| L-aġġettiv irid jaqbel mal-ġens tan-nom ukoll. | The adjective must agree with the gender of the noun as well.<br>**irid** must<br>**jaqbel** agree<br>**mal-ġens** with the gender, ma' + the definite article<br>**tan-nom** of the noun<br>**ukoll** too. |
| Tifel kbir – tifla kbira | A big boy – a big girl.<br>**tifel / tifla** boy / girl<br>**kbir / kbira** big, masculine / feminine. |
| Raġel xiħ – mara xiħa | An old man – an old woman.<br>**raġel / mara** man / woman<br>**xiħ / xiħa** old, masculine / feminine. |
| Kowt iswed – beritta sewda | A black coat – a black cap.<br>**kowt / beritta** coat / cap<br>**iswed / sewda** black, masculine / feminine; the feminine does not simply add a. |
| Meta tqabbel ħaġa ma' oħra tuża l-komparattiv. | When you compare one thing with another you use the comparative.<br>**Meta** when<br>**tqabbel** you compare<br>**ħaġa** thing<br>**ma' oħra** with another<br>**tuża** you use<br>**l-komparattiv** the comparative. |
| Meta tqabbel iktar minn żewġ oġġetti flimkien, tuża s-superlattiv. | The textbook says: when comparing more than two objects together, use the superlative. What actually matters is expressing "the most …" within a range, not mechanically counting objects.<br>**iktar minn** more than<br>**żewġ oġġetti** two objects<br>**flimkien** together<br>**s-superlattiv** the superlative. |

Picture note: **kbir → ikbar → l-ikbar** = big → bigger → biggest. The comparative often goes with **minn** (than), and the superlative is usually defined by **fil-klassi / minn kulħadd** (in the class / of everybody). The textbook's **P.eż.** = pereżempju, "for example".

### Analytic Comparison

`Il-komparattiv u s-superlattiv mhux dejjem jinħadmu b'dan il-mod. Xi aġġettivi jeħtieġu ż-żieda tal-kelma…`

Translation: the comparative and the superlative are not always formed this way; some adjectives need an extra word.

**Il-komparattiv u s-superlattiv** the comparative and the superlative.<br>**mhux dejjem** not always.<br>**jinħadmu** are formed.<br>**b'dan il-mod** in this way.<br>**Xi aġġettivi** some adjectives.<br>**jeħtieġu** need.<br>**ż-żieda** the addition.<br>**tal-kelma** of a word.

| Masculine | Feminine | Comparative (suggested fill-in) | Superlative (suggested fill-in) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bravu | brava | iktar bravu / brava | l-iktar bravu / brava | capable, good at something |
| intelliġenti | intelliġenti | iktar intelliġenti | l-iktar intelliġenti | intelligent; same form for both genders |
| stupidu | stupida | iktar stupidu / stupida | l-iktar stupidu / stupida | stupid; applying it to a person can be offensive |
| modern | moderna | iktar modern / moderna | l-iktar modern / moderna | modern |
| sinjur | sinjura | iktar sinjur / sinjura | l-iktar sinjur / sinjura | rich; here not the title "Mr / Madam" |
| abjad | bajda | iktar abjad / bajda | l-iktar abjad / bajda | white |
| ċar | ċara | iktar ċar / ċara | l-iktar ċar / ċara | clear, light in colour |
| skur | skura | iktar skur / skura | l-iktar skur / skura | dark, dim |

**iktar / iżjed** both mean "more"; **l-iktar / l-iżjed** both mean "most". Here the textbook's analytic exercise is followed; it does not mean these words exclude other comparative expressions in every context.

## 5 The Past Tense: Seventeen Complete Sentences

`Il-Verbi fil-Passat` the verbs in the past tense. **Semitiċi** of Semitic origin; **li ġejjin mill-Għarbi** coming from Arabic. The exercise **Ikteb sentenza b'dawn il-verbi** = write one sentence with each of these verbs; **Ikteb** write; **sentenza** sentence; **b'dawn** with these.

The form given at the head of each item is the third person masculine singular past, "he …ed", not an infinitive or an imperative. The model sentences keep that form.

|---|---|

## 7 The Task and the 56 Conjugated Forms

`Issa agħżel erba' verbi Semitiċi u erbgħa oħra li mhumiex, u kkonjugahom fil-persuni kollha fil-passat`: now choose four Semitic verbs and four others that are not, and conjugate them in all persons in the past.

**Issa** now.<br>**agħżel** choose.<br>**erba' verbi** four verbs.<br>**u erbgħa oħra** and four others, where the number stands alone.<br>**li mhumiex** that are not of this group.<br>**ikkonjugahom** conjugate them, -hom = them.<br>**fil-persuni kollha** in all persons.<br>**fil-passat** in the past.

| Person | kiteb write | fetaħ open | ħasel wash | għamel do, make |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jien I | ktibt | ftaħt | ħsilt | għamilt |
| Int you | ktibt | ftaħt | ħsilt | għamilt |
| Huwa he | kiteb | fetaħ | ħasel | għamel |
| Hija she | kitbet | fetħet | ħaslet | għamlet |
| Aħna we | ktibna | ftaħna | ħsilna | għamilna |
| Intom you (plural) | ktibtu | ftaħtu | ħsiltu | għamiltu |
| Huma they | kitbu | fetħu | ħaslu | għamlu |

| Person | aċċetta accept | ibbukkja book | ipparkja park | studja study |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jien I | aċċettajt | ibbukkjajt | ipparkjajt | studjajt |
| Int you | aċċettajt | ibbukkjajt | ipparkjajt | studjajt |
| Huwa he | aċċetta | ibbukkja | ipparkja | studja |
| Hija she | aċċettat | ibbukkjat | ipparkjat | studjat |
| Aħna we | aċċettajna | ibbukkjajna | ipparkjajna | studjajna |
| Intom you (plural) | aċċettajtu | ibbukkjajtu | ipparkjajtu | studjajtu |
| Huma they | aċċettaw | ibbukkjaw | ipparkjaw | studjaw |

The first and second person singular are often identical and must be told apart by the subject and the context; for example **ktibt** = I wrote / you wrote. **-na** usually marks "we" and **-tu** "you (plural)", but endings cannot simply be glued onto every base form; note **kiteb → ktibna** and **għamel → għamilna**. The third person plural often ends in **-u / -w**. The two tables hold eight verbs with seven persons each.

## 12 Matching the Means of Transport

Exercise `Qabbel il-mezz ta' trasport li jintuża f'din is-sitwazzjoni`: match the means of transport used in this situation. **Qabbel** match; **il-mezz ta' trasport** the means of transport; **li jintuża** that is used; **f'din is-sitwazzjoni** in this situation.

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| rota | bicycle |
| ferrovija | train |
| taxi | taxi |
| vapur | ship, boat |
| tal-linja | bus; the full form is karozza tal-linja |
| ajruplan | plane |
| karozza | car |
| vann | van |
