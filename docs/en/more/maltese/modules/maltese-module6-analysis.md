## 3 Negation: the Rule and Every Example

### The negative pattern and the rules given in the textbook

`Il-Grammatika: In-Negattiv` = grammar: the negative. The core frame is **ma + verb + x**; **m’** is the contracted form of ma, and **x** attaches to the verb or to the verb-plus-suffix group.

| Original | Chinese and word-by-word breakdown |
|---|---|
| Ma nafx. | I do not know.<br>**Ma … x** the negative frame<br>**naf** I know<br>**nafx** naf plus x, not an English-style separate "not". |
| Ma rridx. | I do not want.<br>**Ma … x** negation<br>**rrid** I want, the positive form is often irrid<br>**rridx** the form inside the negative. |
| M’għamilx. | He did not do it.<br>**M’** ma contracted<br>**għamilx** the negative form of għamel, note the vowel. |
| M’għedtlix. | You did not tell me.<br>**M’** ma contracted<br>**għedt** the past "I / you said" share one form; here the second person is taken from the textbook and the "to me" context<br>**-li** to me<br>**-x** the negative, placed after the pronoun suffix. |
| Il-verbi, li huma kliem li juru azzjoni jew stat, jistgħu juru n-negattiv, juri li l-azzjoni ma seħħitx. | The textbook sentence is loose in syntax; it means verbs name an action or a state and can also form the negative, showing that the action did not happen.<br>**Il-verbi** the verbs<br>**li huma kliem** which are words<br>**li juru** that show<br>**azzjoni jew stat** action or state<br>**jistgħu juru n-negattiv** they can show the negative<br>**juri li** shows that<br>**l-azzjoni ma seħħitx** the action did not take place. |
| Normalment, in-negattiv isir billi quddiem il-verb tiżdied il-‘ma’ u wara l-verb tingħaqad ix-x. Eżempju: | Normally the negative is made by adding ma before the verb and joining x after the verb. Example:<br>**Normalment** normally<br>**in-negattiv isir** the negative is made<br>**billi** by<br>**quddiem il-verb** before the verb<br>**tiżdied il-ma** ma is added<br>**u wara l-verb** and after the verb<br>**tingħaqad ix-x** x is joined<br>**Eżempju** example. |
| Meta l-verb jispiċċa bil-vokali ‘a’ fin-negattiv din tinbidel f’ ‘ie’. | The textbook says a verb ending in a changes to ie in the negative; this is a simplified local rule and must be checked against the examples.<br>**Meta** when<br>**l-verb jispiċċa** the verb ends<br>**bil-vokali a** in the vowel a<br>**fin-negattiv** in the negative<br>**din tinbidel f’ ie** it changes to ie. |
| Il-verb li jispiċċa bl-appostrofi f’tarf il-kelma, sinjal li l-kelma għandha l-għajn, fin-negattiv l-appostrofu jaqa’. | A final apostrophe is linked to the historical għ; in the negative the apostrophe is dropped. This is about the spelling of these verbs, not about deleting every apostrophe inside a word.<br>**li jispiċċa bl-appostrofi** that ends with an apostrophe<br>**f’tarf il-kelma** at the end of the word<br>**sinjal** a sign<br>**li l-kelma għandha l-għajn** that the word has għ<br>**fin-negattiv** in the negative<br>**l-appostrofu jaqa’** the apostrophe drops. |

## 5 The Definite Article: Everything Filled In

`L-Artiklu – Imla l-vojt bl-artiklu li jixraq` = the article — fill the blank with the suitable article. **l-artiklu** the article<br>**Imla l-vojt** fill the blank<br>**bl-artiklu** with the article<br>**li jixraq** that fits. `Ikteb l-artiklu quddiem in-nomi` = write the article before the nouns; **quddiem** before<br>**in-nomi** the nouns.

The basic form is **il-**; before a vowel it is often **l-**; before some consonants it is assimilated, e.g. **it-tadam, iż-żebbuġ, is-salamun**. When the preceding word ends in a vowel the initial i can be dropped, e.g. **nitfa’ t-tadam**. Prepositions contract with the article: **minn + il- → mill-**, **bi + il- → bil- / bl-**. Do not confuse ħ and ġ with other letters.
