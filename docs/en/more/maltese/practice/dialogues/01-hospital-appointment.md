# Making a Hospital Appointment

**Scenario:** David calls the health centre to book a doctor's appointment with receptionist Maria and confirm the time and required documents.

## Complete Dialogue

**Maria:** Bonġu, Ċentru tas-Saħħa. Kif nista' ngħinek?

**David:** Bonġu. Nixtieq nagħmel appuntament mat-tabib, jekk jogħġbok.

**Maria:** Għandek preferenza għal xi ġurnata?

**David:** Jekk jista' jkun, għada filgħodu.

**Maria:** Għandna ħin fl-għaxra u nofs. Tajjeb għalik?

**David:** Iva, tajjeb ħafna. Għandi bżonn inġib xi dokumenti?

**Maria:** Ġib il-karta tal-identità u l-karta tas-saħħa.

**David:** Grazzi. Narak għada.

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

### 1. Maria (Receptionist)

**Malti:** Bonġu, Ċentru tas-Saħħa. Kif nista' ngħinek?

**Translation:** Good morning, Health Centre. How can I help you?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bonġu | hello; good morning | Common greeting |
| Ċentru | centre | Masculine noun |
| tas-Saħħa | of health; health | Contracted form of `ta' + is-saħħa`; together, “Health Centre” |
| Kif | how | Question word |
| nista' | I can | First-person marker `n-`; `jista'` means “can/be able” |
| ngħinek | help you | `ngħin` “I help” + `-ek` “you” |

### 2. David (Patient)

**Malti:** Bonġu. Nixtieq nagħmel appuntament mat-tabib, jekk jogħġbok.

**Translation:** Good morning. I would like to make an appointment with the doctor, please.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bonġu | hello; good morning | Greeting |
| Nixtieq | I would like | Polite expression of a wish |
| nagħmel | I do; I make; I arrange | `nagħmel appuntament` means “make an appointment” |
| appuntament | appointment | Masculine loanword |
| mat-tabib | with the doctor; at the doctor's | `ma'` “with” + definite article, contracted in spelling |
| jekk | if | Used with `jogħġbok` in a polite request |
| jogħġbok | please; may it please you | `jekk jogħġbok` as a whole means “please” |

### 3. Maria (Receptionist)

**Malti:** Għandek preferenza għal xi ġurnata?

**Translation:** Do you have a preferred day?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għandek | you have | Second-person singular form of `għand` |
| preferenza | preference; choice | Feminine noun |
| għal | for; regarding | Preposition |
| xi | some; any; a certain | Used here to ask which day |
| ġurnata | day | Feminine noun |

### 4. David (Patient)

**Malti:** Jekk jista' jkun, għada filgħodu.

**Translation:** If possible, tomorrow morning.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jekk | if | Conjunction |
| jista' | can; may | Third-person masculine singular |
| jkun | be; become | Subjunctive/subordinate form used in the expression `jekk jista' jkun` |
| għada | tomorrow | Time adverb |
| filgħodu | in the morning | Time expression |

### 5. Maria (Receptionist)

**Malti:** Għandna ħin fl-għaxra u nofs. Tajjeb għalik?

**Translation:** We have an opening at half past ten. Does that work for you?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għandna | we have | First-person plural form of `għand` |
| ħin | time; free time | Masculine noun |
| fl-għaxra | at ten | Contracted `f'` “at” + `l-għaxra` “ten o'clock” |
| u | and; past | Connects the hour and “half” |
| nofs | half | Used for half past the hour |
| Tajjeb | good; suitable | Masculine form; refers here to the time |
| għalik | for you | `għal` “for” + `-ik` “you” |

### 6. David (Patient)

**Malti:** Iva, tajjeb ħafna. Għandi bżonn inġib xi dokumenti?

**Translation:** Yes, that works very well. Do I need to bring any documents?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Iva | yes | Affirmative response |
| tajjeb | good; suitable | Masculine form |
| ħafna | very; a lot | Modifies `tajjeb` here: “very” |
| Għandi | I have | First-person singular form of `għand` |
| bżonn | need | `għandi bżonn` means “I need” |
| inġib | I bring; I take along | First-person singular form |
| xi | some; any | Can mean “what” in a question |
| dokumenti | documents | Plural of `dokument` |

### 7. Maria (Receptionist)

**Malti:** Ġib il-karta tal-identità u l-karta tas-saħħa.

**Translation:** Bring your identity card and health card.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ġib | bring | Singular imperative |
| il-karta | the card; paper | Definite article `il-` + `karta` |
| tal-identità | of identity; identity | Contracted form of `ta' + l-identità` |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| il-karta | the card | Feminine noun with the definite article |
| tas-saħħa | of health; health | Contracted form of `ta' + is-saħħa` |

### 8. David (Patient)

**Malti:** Grazzi. Narak għada.

**Translation:** Thank you. See you tomorrow.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Grazzi | thank you | Common polite expression |
| Narak | I see you; see you | `nara` “I see” + `-k` “you” |
| għada | tomorrow | Time adverb |

## Useful Variations

- `filgħodu`: in the morning; `wara nofsinhar`: in the afternoon; `filgħaxija`: in the evening.
- `Għandi appuntament.`: I have an appointment.
- `Nista' nbiddel il-ħin?`: Can I change the time?
