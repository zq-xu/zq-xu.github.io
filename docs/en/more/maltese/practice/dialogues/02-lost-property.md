# Lost Property and Collection at the Police Station

**Scenario:** Mark answers Anna's call at the police station. They identify her wallet and arrange a collection time.

## Complete Dialogue

**Anna:** Bonġu. Sibtu kartiera mitlufa ħdejn il-waqfa tal-karozzi tal-linja?

**Mark:** Iva, il-kartiera tinsab hawn fl-għassa tal-pulizija.

**Anna:** X'nagħmel biex niġi niġborha?

**Mark:** Tista' tiġi l-għassa għada filgħodu.

**Anna:** Nista' niġi fid-disgħa?

**Mark:** Iva. Ġib miegħek dokument ta' identità.

**Anna:** Se jkolli bżonn inġib xi prova oħra?

**Mark:** Le, id-dokument tal-identità jkun biżżejjed.

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

### 1. Anna (Owner of the Lost Item)

**Malti:** Bonġu. Sibtu kartiera mitlufa ħdejn il-waqfa tal-karozzi tal-linja?

**Translation:** Hello. Did you find a lost wallet near the bus stop?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bonġu | hello | Greeting |
| Sibtu | did you find it? | `sibt` is the find-form interpreted from context; `-u` can refer to a masculine object (“it”) |
| kartiera | wallet | Feminine noun |
| mitlufa | lost | Feminine singular form agreeing with `kartiera` |
| ħdejn | beside; near | Preposition of location |
| il-waqfa | the stop | Feminine noun with the definite article |
| tal-karozzi | of the cars | `ta'` contracts with the article; `karozzi` is plural |
| tal-linja | of the line | Contracted `ta' + il-linja`; `karozzi tal-linja` means “bus” |

### 2. Mark (Police Officer)

**Malti:** Iva, il-kartiera tinsab hawn fl-għassa tal-pulizija.

**Translation:** Yes, the wallet is here at the police station.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Iva | yes | Affirmative response |
| il-kartiera | the wallet | Definite article + `kartiera` |
| tinsab | is located; is | Form used with a feminine subject |
| hawn | here | Place adverb |
| fl-għassa | at the station | Contracted `f'` “at” + `l-għassa` |
| tal-pulizija | of the police | Contracted `ta' + il-pulizija` |

### 3. Anna (Owner of the Lost Item)

**Malti:** X'nagħmel biex niġi niġborha?

**Translation:** What should I do to come and collect it?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| X' | what; how | Short form of `xiex` |
| nagħmel | I do | First-person singular form |
| biex | in order to; so that | Introduces a purpose or following action |
| niġi | I come | First-person singular form |
| niġborha | I collect it; retrieve it | `niġbor` “I collect” + `-ha` “it” (feminine) |

### 4. Mark (Police Officer)

**Malti:** Tista' tiġi l-għassa għada filgħodu.

**Translation:** You can come to the station tomorrow morning.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tista' | you can | Second-person singular form |
| tiġi | come | Used with `tista'` to mean “can come” |
| l-għassa | the station; police station | Feminine noun with the definite article |
| għada | tomorrow | Time adverb |
| filgħodu | in the morning | Time expression |

### 5. Anna (Owner of the Lost Item)

**Malti:** Nista' niġi fid-disgħa?

**Translation:** Can I come at nine?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nista' | can I? | First-person singular form |
| niġi | I come | First-person singular form |
| fid-disgħa | at nine | Contracted `f'` “at” + `id-disgħa` “nine o'clock” |

### 6. Mark (Police Officer)

**Malti:** Iva. Ġib miegħek dokument ta' identità.

**Translation:** Yes. Bring an identity document with you.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Iva | yes; all right | Affirmative response |
| Ġib | bring | Singular imperative |
| miegħek | with you; along with you | `miegħ` “with” + `-ek` “you” |
| dokument | document; identification | Masculine noun |
| ta' | of | Connects nouns to show type or possession |
| identità | identity | Feminine noun |

### 7. Anna (Owner of the Lost Item)

**Malti:** Se jkolli bżonn inġib xi prova oħra?

**Translation:** Will I need to bring any other proof?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Se | will | Common future marker |
| jkolli | I will have; I will need | One future form of `għandi` |
| bżonn | need | `ikolli bżonn` means “I need” |
| inġib | I bring | First-person singular form |
| xi | some; any | Means “any other” here |
| prova | proof; evidence | Feminine noun |
| oħra | other | Feminine form agreeing with `prova` |

### 8. Mark (Police Officer)

**Malti:** Le, id-dokument tal-identità jkun biżżejjed.

**Translation:** No, the identity document will be enough.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Le | no | Negative response |
| id-dokument | the document | Definite article written with the noun |
| tal-identità | of identity; identity | Contracted `ta' + l-identità` |
| jkun | will be; may be | Subjunctive/subordinate form of `ikun` |
| biżżejjed | enough; sufficient | Fixed adjective/adverb expression |

## Additional Vocabulary

- `uffiċċju tal-pulizija`: police office.
- `rapport`: report; incident report.
- `Tlift il-kartiera tiegħi.`: I lost my wallet.
- Actual procedures for collecting lost property vary by station and item; this dialogue is for language practice.
