# Going to a Weekend Party

**Scenario:** Two friends make plans for a weekend party.

## Complete Dialogue

**Anna:** Se tagħmel xi ħaġa fi tmiem il-ġimgħa?

**Mark:** Għadni ma ddeċidejtx. Għaliex?

**Anna:** Qed norganizza festa d-dar is-Sibt filgħaxija.

**Mark:** Ħsejjes sbieħ! F'liema ħin tibda?

**Anna:** Nibdew fit-tmienja, imma tista' tasal aktar tard.

**Mark:** X'għandi nġib miegħi?

**Anna:** Ġib xi ħaġa x'tixorbu u jien nagħmel l-ikel.

**Mark:** Perfett. Inkun hemm fit-tmienja.

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

### 1. Anna

**Malti:** Se tagħmel xi ħaġa fi tmiem il-ġimgħa?

**Translation:** Are you doing anything this weekend?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Se | will; going to | Used with the imperfect to ask about plans |
| tagħmel | you do; you make | Second-person singular form |
| xi | some; any | Used to ask whether there are plans |
| ħaġa | thing | Feminine noun |
| fi | in; at | Preposition used with time expressions |
| tmiem | end | Masculine noun |
| il-ġimgħa | the week | `tmiem il-ġimgħa` as a whole means “weekend” |

### 2. Mark

**Malti:** Għadni ma ddeċidejtx. Għaliex?

**Translation:** I haven't decided yet. Why?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għadni | I still; I have yet to | `għad` “still” + first-person suffix `-ni` |
| ma | not | Part of the negative construction, commonly paired with final `x` |
| ddeċidejtx | I did not decide; I haven't decided | Negative perfect form; final `-x` marks negation |
| Għaliex | why | Question word |

### 3. Anna

**Malti:** Qed norganizza festa d-dar is-Sibt filgħaxija.

**Translation:** I'm organizing a party at home on Saturday evening.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Qed | currently; in the process of | Combines with an imperfect verb to express an ongoing action |
| norganizza | I organize | First-person singular form |
| festa | party; celebration | Feminine noun |
| d-dar | at home; the house | Common contracted spelling of `id-dar` |
| is-Sibt | Saturday | Often used with the definite article |
| filgħaxija | in the evening | Time expression |

### 4. Mark

**Malti:** Ħsejjes sbieħ! F'liema ħin tibda?

**Translation:** Sounds nice! What time does it start?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ħsejjes | it sounds | Third-person form, related literally to “sounds” |
| sbieħ | nice; pleasant | Masculine plural form/fixed expression |
| F'liema | at what; in which | `f'` “at” + `liema` “which/what” |
| ħin | time | Masculine noun |
| tibda | you start; it starts | The form depends on the subject; here it refers to the party |

### 5. Anna

**Malti:** Nibdew fit-tmienja, imma tista' tasal aktar tard.

**Translation:** We'll start at eight, but you can arrive later.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nibdew | we start | First-person plural form |
| fit-tmienja | at eight | Contracted `f'` + definite clock-hour form |
| imma | but | Conjunction |
| tista' | you can | Second-person singular form |
| tasal | you arrive | Second-person singular form |
| aktar | more; later | Comparative adverb |
| tard | late | Adverb/adjective |

### 6. Mark

**Malti:** X'għandi nġib miegħi?

**Translation:** What should I bring with me?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| X' | what | Shortened question word |
| għandi | I have; should I | With the following verb, it expresses “what should I…” |
| nġib | I bring | First-person singular form |
| miegħi | with me; along with me | `miegħ` “with” + `-i` “me” |

### 7. Anna

**Malti:** Ġib xi ħaġa x'tixorbu u jien nagħmel l-ikel.

**Translation:** Bring something to drink, and I'll prepare the food.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ġib | bring | Singular imperative |
| xi | some | Indefinite determiner |
| ħaġa | thing | Feminine noun |
| x' | to; that can be | Introduces a clause describing the preceding noun |
| tixorbu | you drink (plural); everyone drinks | Plural form, referring here to the guests |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| jien | I | Emphatic pronoun |
| nagħmel | I do; I make; I prepare | First-person singular form |
| l-ikel | the food | Definite article + `ikel` |

### 8. Mark

**Malti:** Perfett. Inkun hemm fit-tmienja.

**Translation:** Perfect. I'll be there at eight.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Perfett | perfect; great | Masculine adjective, also used as an exclamation |
| Inkun | I will be | First-person singular form |
| hemm | there; present | Place adverb |
| fit-tmienja | at eight | Time expression |

## Conversational Alternatives

- `il-Ġimgħa filgħaxija`: Friday evening.
- `Nista' nġib deżerta?`: Can I bring dessert?
- `Narawk is-Sibt.`: See you on Saturday.
