# Asking the Time

**Scenario:** Two friends meet at an agreed place. One asks the time and checks whether there is still time before an appointment.

## Complete Dialogue

**Mark:** Skużani, x'ħin hu issa?

**Lina:** Huma t-tmienja u kwart.

**Mark:** Grazzi. Għandi appuntament fit-tmienja u nofs.

**Lina:** Għad għandek ħmistax-il minuta.

**Mark:** Tajjeb. Il-post jinsab ħdejn il-pjazza, hux?

**Lina:** Iva, eżattament. Jien qiegħda quddiem il-bieb.

**Mark:** Qed nitlaq issa. Nasal f'għaxar minuti.

**Lina:** Sewwa, narak dalwaqt.

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

### 1. Mark

**Malti:** Skużani, x'ħin hu issa?

**Translation:** Excuse me, what time is it now?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Skużani | excuse me; forgive me | Polite expression |
| x'ħin | what time | `x'` “what” + `ħin` “time” |
| hu | is | Third-person masculine pronoun, used here in the time expression |
| issa | now | Time adverb |

### 2. Lina

**Malti:** Huma t-tmienja u kwart.

**Translation:** It is quarter past eight.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Huma | they are; it is | Copula-like form introducing the time |
| it-tmienja | eight o'clock | Clock-hour form with the definite article |
| u | and; past | Connects the hour and minutes |
| kwart | quarter | A quarter of an hour; fifteen minutes |

### 3. Mark

**Malti:** Grazzi. Għandi appuntament fit-tmienja u nofs.

**Translation:** Thanks. I have an appointment at half past eight.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Grazzi | thank you; thanks | Polite expression |
| Għandi | I have | First-person singular form of `għand` |
| appuntament | appointment | Masculine noun |
| fit-tmienja | at eight | Contracted `f'` “at” + clock-hour form |
| u | and; past | Connects parts of the time expression |
| nofs | half | `u nofs` means “half past” |

### 4. Lina

**Malti:** Għad għandek ħmistax-il minuta.

**Translation:** You still have fifteen minutes.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għad | still; yet | Indicates continuation |
| għandek | you have | Second-person singular form of `għand` |
| ħmistax-il | fifteen | Numeral form linking to the following noun |
| minuta | minute | Feminine noun |

### 5. Mark

**Malti:** Tajjeb. Il-post jinsab ħdejn il-pjazza, hux?

**Translation:** All right. The place is next to the square, isn't it?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tajjeb | good; all right | Masculine form, also used as a response |
| il-post | the place | Definite article + `post` |
| jinsab | is located; is | Third-person masculine singular form |
| ħdejn | beside; next to | Preposition of location |
| il-pjazza | the square | Feminine noun with the definite article |
| hux | isn't it?; right? | Tag question used for confirmation |

### 6. Lina

**Malti:** Iva, eżattament. Jien qiegħda quddiem il-bieb.

**Translation:** Yes, exactly. I'm in front of the door.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Iva | yes | Affirmative response |
| eżattament | exactly | Adverb |
| Jien | I | Emphatic pronoun |
| qiegħda | located; situated | Feminine speaker form; a male speaker uses `qiegħed` |
| quddiem | in front of | Preposition of location |
| il-bieb | the door | Masculine noun with the definite article |

### 7. Mark

**Malti:** Qed nitlaq issa. Nasal f'għaxar minuti.

**Translation:** I'm leaving now. I'll arrive in ten minutes.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Qed | currently; in the process of | Used with a verb to express an ongoing action |
| nitlaq | I leave; I set off | First-person singular form |
| issa | now | Time adverb |
| Nasal | I arrive | First-person singular form |
| f'għaxar | in ten | `f'` + `għaxar` “ten” |
| minuti | minutes | Plural of `minuta` |

### 8. Lina

**Malti:** Sewwa, narak dalwaqt.

**Translation:** All right, see you soon.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sewwa | all right; okay | Common response |
| narak | I see you; see you | `nara` “I see” + `-k` “you” |
| dalwaqt | soon; shortly | Time adverb |

## Time Expressions

- `u kwart`: quarter past; `u nofs`: half past.
- `neqsin kwart`: quarter to the next hour, for example `id-disgħa neqsin kwart` (quarter to nine, 8:45).
- Use 24-hour numeric time when you need to avoid ambiguity.
