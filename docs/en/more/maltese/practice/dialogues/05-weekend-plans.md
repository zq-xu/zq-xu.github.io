# Talking About Upcoming Plans

**Scenario:** Anna and Mark talk about plans for today, tomorrow, this weekend, next week, next month, and next year.

## Complete Dialogue

**Anna:** X'għandek tagħmel illum u għada?

**Mark:** Illum naħdem mid-dar u filgħaxija nistudja l-Malti. Għada se mmur l-isptar ma' ommi.

**Anna:** X'se tagħmel fi tmiem il-ġimgħa?

**Mark:** Se niltaqa' mal-ħbieb għal kafè.

**Anna:** Għandek pjanijiet għall-ġimgħa d-dieħla?

**Mark:** Iva. Nhar it-Tlieta se nżur lill-familja tiegħi.

**Anna:** U x-xahar id-dieħel?

**Mark:** Se nivvjaġġa lejn Għawdex għal ftit jiem.

**Anna:** X'tixtieq tagħmel is-sena d-dieħla?

**Mark:** Nixtieq nitgħallem aktar il-Malti u nsiefer.

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

### 1. Anna

**Malti:** X'għandek tagħmel illum u għada?

**Translation:** What are you doing today and tomorrow?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| X' | what | Short form of `xiex`, often attached to the following word |
| għandek | you have; you need to | Second-person singular form of `għand` |
| tagħmel | you do; you make | Second-person singular form |
| illum | today | Time adverb |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| għada | tomorrow | Time adverb |

### 2. Mark

**Malti:** Illum naħdem mid-dar u filgħaxija nistudja l-Malti.

**Translation:** Today I work from home, and in the evening I study Maltese.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Illum | today | Time adverb |
| naħdem | I work | First-person singular form |
| mid-dar | from home; at home | Contracted form of `minn` “from” + `id-dar` “the house” |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| filgħaxija | in the evening | Time expression |
| nistudja | I study | First-person singular form |
| l-Malti | the Maltese language | Language name with the definite article |

### 3. Mark

**Malti:** Għada se mmur l-isptar ma' ommi.

**Translation:** Tomorrow I'll go to the hospital with my mother.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għada | tomorrow | Time adverb |
| se | will; going to | Common future marker |
| mmur | I go | First-person singular form |
| l-isptar | the hospital | Definite article + `isptar` |
| ma' | with | Preposition |
| ommi | my mother | `omm` “mother” + first-person possessive suffix `-i` |

### 4. Anna

**Malti:** X'se tagħmel fi tmiem il-ġimgħa?

**Translation:** What are you going to do this weekend?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| X' | what | Short question form |
| se | will; going to | Future marker |
| tagħmel | you do; you make | Second-person singular form |
| fi | in; at | Preposition |
| tmiem | end | Combines with the following words in a time expression |
| il-ġimgħa | the week | `tmiem il-ġimgħa` as a whole means “weekend” |

### 5. Mark

**Malti:** Se niltaqa' mal-ħbieb għal kafè.

**Translation:** I'll meet my friends for coffee.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Se | will; going to | Future marker |
| niltaqa' | I meet | First-person singular form; the apostrophe is part of the spelling |
| mal-ħbieb | with the friends | Contracted `ma'` “with” + `il-ħbieb` “the friends” |
| għal | for | Expresses purpose here |
| kafè | coffee | Masculine noun; refers here to having coffee |

### 6. Anna

**Malti:** Għandek pjanijiet għall-ġimgħa d-dieħla?

**Translation:** Do you have plans for next week?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għandek | you have | Second-person singular form of `għand` |
| pjanijiet | plans | Plural of `pjan` |
| għall-ġimgħa | for the week | Contracted `għal` + `il-ġimgħa` |
| d-dieħla | coming; next | Feminine form agreeing with `ġimgħa` |

### 7. Mark

**Malti:** Iva. Nhar it-Tlieta se nżur lill-familja tiegħi.

**Translation:** Yes. On Tuesday I'll visit my family.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Iva | yes | Affirmative response |
| Nhar | on the day of | Introduces a weekday or date |
| it-Tlieta | Tuesday | Weekday commonly used with the definite article |
| se | will; going to | Future marker |
| nżur | I visit | First-person singular form |
| lill-familja | the family (as object) | Personal object marker `lil` written with the definite article |
| tiegħi | my; mine | Possessive pronoun |

### 8. Anna

**Malti:** U x-xahar id-dieħel?

**Translation:** And what about next month?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| U | and; so | Connects to the previous topic or introduces a follow-up |
| x-xahar | the month; this month | Definite form of `xahar` “month” |
| id-dieħel | coming; next | Masculine form agreeing with `xahar` |

### 9. Mark

**Malti:** Se nivvjaġġa lejn Għawdex għal ftit jiem.

**Translation:** I'll travel to Gozo for a few days next month.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Se | will; going to | Future marker; “next month” is understood from the question |
| nivvjaġġa | I travel | First-person singular form |
| lejn | toward; to | Directional preposition |
| Għawdex | Gozo | Island in the Maltese archipelago |
| għal | for; lasting | Introduces a duration here |
| ftit | a few; a little | Quantifier |
| jiem | days | Plural of `jum` “day” |

### 10. Anna

**Malti:** X'tixtieq tagħmel is-sena d-dieħla?

**Translation:** What would you like to do next year?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| X' | what | Short question form |
| tixtieq | you would like; you want | Second-person singular form, often used for polite questions |
| tagħmel | you do; you make | Second-person singular form |
| is-sena | the year | Definite form of `sena` “year” |
| d-dieħla | coming; next | Feminine form agreeing with `sena` |

### 11. Mark

**Malti:** Nixtieq nitgħallem aktar il-Malti u nsiefer.

**Translation:** I'd like to learn more Maltese and travel abroad.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nixtieq | I would like; I want | First-person singular form |
| nitgħallem | I learn | First-person singular form; contains medial `għ` |
| aktar | more; further | Adverb of degree |
| il-Malti | the Maltese language | Language name |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| nsiefer | I travel abroad | First-person singular form |

## Quick Reference: Time Expressions

- `illum`: today
- `għada`: tomorrow
- `fi tmiem il-ġimgħa`: this weekend
- `il-ġimgħa d-dieħla`: next week
- `ix-xahar id-dieħel`: next month
- `is-sena d-dieħla`: next year
