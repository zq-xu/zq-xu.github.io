# Dinner at the Restaurant — Text Messages

**Scenario:** Claire and her friend exchange text messages to confirm tonight's dinner at a restaurant: the meeting time, what to wear, and childcare arrangements. (Speaker labels are inferred from the dialogue context.)

## Complete Dialogue

**Friend:** Waranofsinhar it-tajjeb, Claire. Għal illejla kollox sew?

**Claire:** Waranofsinhar it-tajjeb. Iva għal-lejla kollox sew. Fit-tmienja u nofs hux?

**Friend:** Eżatt, niltaqgħu fit-tmienja u nofs u nidħlu ġewwa l-ħanut.

**Friend:** X'ser tilbes?

**Friend:** Ħa tilbes ġakketta?

**Claire:** Qed ikun ftit bard u għalhekk naħseb nilbes ġakketta u flokk tas-suf.

**Friend:** Tajjeb, nilbes bħalek mela. It-tifel se iġġibuh magħkom?

**Claire:** Jien u r-raġel, it-tifel ser inħalluh m'ommi.

**Friend:** Tajjeb, niltaqgħu mela. Saħħiet.

**Claire:** Iva, niltaqgħu. Saħħiet.

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

### 1. Friend

**Malti:** Waranofsinhar it-tajjeb, Claire. Għal illejla kollox sew?

**Translation:** Good afternoon, Claire. Is everything all right for tonight?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Waranofsinhar | afternoon | `wara` (after) + `nofsinhar` (noon) |
| it-tajjeb | the good | `tajjeb` with the definite article; greeting phrase "good afternoon" |
| Għal | for | Preposition |
| illejla | tonight | `il-lejla` |
| kollox | everything | Indefinite pronoun |
| sew | fine; well | Adverb |

### 2. Claire

**Malti:** Waranofsinhar it-tajjeb. Iva għal-lejla kollox sew. Fit-tmienja u nofs hux?

**Translation:** Good afternoon. Yes, everything is fine for tonight. Half past eight, right?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Waranofsinhar it-tajjeb | good afternoon | Greeting phrase |
| Iva | yes | Affirmative response |
| għal-lejla | for tonight | Contracted `għal` + `il-lejla` |
| kollox sew | all fine | Fixed phrase |
| Fit-tmienja | at eight | Contracted `f'` + `it-tmienja` |
| u nofs | and a half | Half past the hour |
| hux | right? isn't it? | `hu` + `x`, a tag question |

### 3. Friend

**Malti:** Eżatt, niltaqgħu fit-tmienja u nofs u nidħlu ġewwa l-ħanut.

**Translation:** Exactly, we'll meet at half past eight and go inside the shop.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Eżatt | exactly | Loanword `exact` |
| niltaqgħu | we meet | First-person plural |
| fit-tmienja u nofs | at half past eight | Time expression |
| nidħlu | we enter | First-person plural |
| ġewwa | inside | Preposition |
| l-ħanut | the shop | Masculine noun with the definite article |

### 4. Friend

**Malti:** X'ser tilbes?

**Translation:** What are you going to wear?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| X' | what | Contracted form of `x` |
| ser | going to | Future marker |
| tilbes | you wear | Second-person singular |

### 5. Friend

**Malti:** Ħa tilbes ġakketta?

**Translation:** Are you going to wear a jacket?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ħa | going to | Colloquial future marker |
| tilbes | you wear | Second-person singular |
| ġakketta | jacket | Loanword |

### 6. Claire

**Malti:** Qed ikun ftit bard u għalhekk naħseb nilbes ġakketta u flokk tas-suf.

**Translation:** It's a bit cold, so I think I'll wear a jacket and a wool sweater.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Qed | is (progressive) | Progressive marker |
| ikun | is; will be | Present of `kun`, used for the weather |
| ftit | a bit | Degree adverb |
| bard | cold | Noun |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| għalhekk | therefore | `għal` + `hekk` |
| naħseb | I think | First-person singular |
| nilbes | I wear | First-person singular |
| flokk | sweater; jersey | Loanword |
| tas-suf | of wool | Contracted `ta'` + `is-suf` |

### 7. Friend

**Malti:** Tajjeb, nilbes bħalek mela. It-tifel se iġġibuh magħkom?

**Translation:** OK, then I'll wear the same as you. Are you bringing the child with you?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tajjeb | OK; fine | Adjective |
| nilbes | I wear | First-person singular |
| bħalek | like you | `bħal` + `-ek` (your) |
| mela | then; so | Discourse marker |
| It-tifel | the child | Masculine noun with the definite article |
| se | will | Future marker |
| iġġibuh | you bring him | `iġġibu` + `-h` (him) |
| magħkom | with you | `ma'` + `-kom` (plural "you") |

### 8. Claire

**Malti:** Jien u r-raġel, it-tifel ser inħalluh m'ommi.

**Translation:** My husband and I, we'll leave the child with my mother.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jien | I | First-person pronoun |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| r-raġel | the husband | `raġel` with the definite article |
| it-tifel | the child | With the definite article |
| ser | will | Future marker |
| inħalluh | we leave him | `inħallu` + `-h` (him) |
| m'ommi | with my mother | Contracted `ma'` + `ommi` (my mother) |

### 9. Friend

**Malti:** Tajjeb, niltaqgħu mela. Saħħiet.

**Translation:** OK, then let's meet. Bye.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tajjeb | OK | Adjective |
| niltaqgħu | we meet | First-person plural |
| mela | then | Discourse marker |
| Saħħiet | bye | Plural farewell from `saħħa` |

### 10. Claire

**Malti:** Iva, niltaqgħu. Saħħiet.

**Translation:** Yes, we'll meet. Bye.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Iva | yes | Affirmative response |
| niltaqgħu | we meet | First-person plural |
| Saħħiet | bye | Farewell |
