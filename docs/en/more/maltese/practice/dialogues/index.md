# Dialogue Index

This category contains eight beginner Maltese dialogues. Each dialogue is presented in full, followed by an English sentence-by-sentence translation and word notes. Numbering starts at 01 within this category.

1. [Making a Hospital Appointment](01-hospital-appointment.md): book a doctor's appointment and confirm the date, time, and required documents.
2. [Lost Property and Collection at the Police Station](02-lost-property.md): report a found wallet and arrange a time to collect it.
3. [Going to a Weekend Party](03-weekend-party.md): discuss the party time, location, and what to bring.
4. [Asking the Time](04-asking-the-time.md): ask the time and confirm an appointment.
5. [Talking About Upcoming Plans](05-weekend-plans.md): discuss plans for today, tomorrow, this weekend, next week, next month, and next year.
6. [Making an Appointment with Dr Farrugia](06-jodie-doctor-appointment.md): Jodie books an appointment with Dr Farrugia for next week and agrees on Thursday evening at seven.
7. [Dinner at the Restaurant — Text Messages](07-claire-restaurant-messages.md): Claire and her friend text to confirm tonight's dinner — the meeting time, what to wear, and childcare arrangements.
8. [The Rented Car](08-rented-car.md): Carmen calls the car-hire company to arrange returning her rented car before flying back to her country on Friday.
