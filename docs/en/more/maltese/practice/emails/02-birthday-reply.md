# Reply to a Birthday Party Invitation

**Scenario:** Mark accepts Anna's birthday invitation and asks whether he can help.

## Full Email

> Għażiża Anna,
>
> Grazzi ħafna tal-istedina.
>
> Iva, nixtieq niġi għall-festa nhar is-Sibt.
>
> Se nkun hemm fis-sebgħa.
>
> Nista' nġib xi ħaġa miegħi?
>
> Nista' nġib kejk żgħir.
>
> Għidli jekk għandek bżonn l-għajnuna.
>
> Nistenna bil-ħerqa li narak.
>
> Tiegħek, Mark

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

**Malti:** Għażiża Anna,

**Translation:** Dear Anna,

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għażiża | dear | Feminine singular form used to address a woman |
| Anna | Anna | Name |

### 1.

**Malti:** Grazzi ħafna tal-istedina.

**Translation:** Thank you very much for the invitation.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Grazzi | thank you | Polite expression |
| ħafna | very; a lot | Adverb of degree |
| tal-istedina | for the invitation; of the invitation | Contracted `ta' + l-istedina` |

### 2.

**Malti:** Iva, nixtieq niġi għall-festa nhar is-Sibt.

**Translation:** Yes, I would like to come to the party on Saturday.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Iva | yes; all right | Affirmative response |
| nixtieq | I would like; I want | Polite expression of a wish |
| niġi | I come | First-person singular form |
| għall-festa | to the party; for the party | Contracted `għal` + `il-festa` |
| nhar | on the day of | Introduces a weekday/date |
| is-Sibt | Saturday | Weekday with the definite article |

### 3.

**Malti:** Se nkun hemm fis-sebgħa.

**Translation:** I will be there at seven.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Se | will; going to | Future marker |
| nkun | I will be | First-person singular form |
| hemm | there; present | Place adverb |
| fis-sebgħa | at seven | Contracted `f'` + definite clock-hour form |

### 4.

**Malti:** Nista' nġib xi ħaġa miegħi?

**Translation:** Can I bring something with me?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nista' | can I? | First-person singular form |
| nġib | I bring | First-person singular form |
| xi | some; a certain | Indefinite determiner |
| ħaġa | thing | Feminine noun |
| miegħi | with me; along with me | `miegħ` “with” + `-i` “me” |

### 5.

**Malti:** Nista' nġib kejk żgħir.

**Translation:** I can bring a small cake.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nista' | I can | First-person singular form |
| nġib | I bring | First-person singular form |
| kejk | cake | Masculine noun |
| żgħir | small | Masculine singular form agreeing with `kejk` |

### 6.

**Malti:** Għidli jekk għandek bżonn l-għajnuna.

**Translation:** Tell me if you need help.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għidli | tell me | `għid` “tell” + `-li` “me” |
| jekk | if | Conjunction |
| għandek | you have | Second-person singular form of `għand` |
| bżonn | need | `għandek bżonn` means “you need” |
| l-għajnuna | the help; help | Feminine noun with the definite article |

### 7.

**Malti:** Nistenna bil-ħerqa li narak.

**Translation:** I look forward to seeing you.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nistenna | I wait; I look forward | First-person singular form |
| bil-ħerqa | eagerly; with anticipation | Fixed adverbial phrase |
| li | to; introduces a clause | Conjunction |
| narak | I see you | `nara` “I see” + `-k` “you” |

**Malti:** Tiegħek, Mark

**Translation:** Yours, Mark.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tiegħek | yours; sincerely | Email closing |
| Mark | Mark | Name |
