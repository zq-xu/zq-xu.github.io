# Wedding Invitation

**Scenario:** A couple invites friends and family to their wedding ceremony and reception.

## Full Email

> Għeżież ħbieb,
>
> Għandna pjaċir nistednukom għat-tieġ tagħna.
>
> Iċ-ċerimonja se ssir nhar is-Sibt, 18 ta' Lulju.
>
> Tibda fil-ħamsa ta' wara nofsinhar fil-knisja ta' San Pawl.
>
> Wara ċ-ċerimonja, se jkun hemm riċeviment fil-lukanda Marina.
>
> Jekk jogħġobkom, ikkonfermaw jekk tistgħux tattendu sal-1 ta' Lulju.
>
> Nistennew bil-ħerqa li niċċelebraw dan il-jum speċjali magħkom.
>
> Dejjem tagħkom, Anna u Mark

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

**Malti:** Għeżież ħbieb,

**Translation:** Dear friends,

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għeżież | dear | Masculine plural form used to address a group |
| ħbieb | friends | Plural of `ħabib` |

### 1.

**Malti:** Għandna pjaċir nistednukom għat-tieġ tagħna.

**Translation:** We are pleased to invite you to our wedding.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għandna | we have | First-person plural form of `għand` |
| pjaċir | pleasure; delight | `għandna pjaċir` means “we are pleased” |
| nistednukom | we invite you (plural) | `nistiednu` “we invite” + `-kom` “you (plural)” |
| għat-tieġ | to the wedding | Contracted `għal` + `it-tieġ` |
| tagħna | our; ours | First-person plural possessive |

### 2.

**Malti:** Iċ-ċerimonja se ssir nhar is-Sibt, 18 ta' Lulju.

**Translation:** The ceremony will take place on Saturday, 18 July.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Iċ-ċerimonja | the ceremony | Feminine noun with the definite article |
| se | will | Future marker |
| ssir | take place; happen | Feminine form of `isir`, agreeing with `ċerimonja` |
| nhar | on the day of | Introduces a weekday/date |
| is-Sibt | Saturday | Weekday with the definite article |
| 18 | eighteen | Day of the month |
| ta' | of | Links the day and month in a date |
| Lulju | July | Month name |

### 3.

**Malti:** Tibda fil-ħamsa ta' wara nofsinhar fil-knisja ta' San Pawl.

**Translation:** It starts at five in the afternoon at St Paul's Church.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tibda | it starts | Third-person feminine singular, referring to the ceremony |
| fil-ħamsa | at five | Contracted `f'` “at” + clock-hour form |
| ta' | of; during | Links the time and part of day |
| wara | after | Combines with `nofsinhar` to mean “afternoon” |
| nofsinhar | midday | `wara nofsinhar` means “afternoon” |
| fil-knisja | at the church | Contracted `f'` + `il-knisja` |
| ta' | of | Links the church and its name |
| San | Saint | Title before a name |
| Pawl | Paul | Name |

### 4.

**Malti:** Wara ċ-ċerimonja, se jkun hemm riċeviment fil-lukanda Marina.

**Translation:** After the ceremony, there will be a reception at the Marina Hotel.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wara | after | Preposition of time |
| ċ-ċerimonja | the ceremony | Refers to `iċ-ċerimonja` in this context |
| se | will | Future marker |
| jkun | be; there will be | Form of `ikun` |
| hemm | there; there is | `se jkun hemm` means “there will be” |
| riċeviment | reception | Masculine loanword |
| fil-lukanda | at the hotel | Contracted `f'` + `il-lukanda` |
| Marina | Marina | Hotel name |

### 5.

**Malti:** Jekk jogħġobkom, ikkonfermaw jekk tistgħux tattendu sal-1 ta' Lulju.

**Translation:** Please confirm by 1 July whether you will be able to attend.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jekk jogħġobkom | please | Polite expression addressed to several people |
| ikkonfermaw | confirm | Plural imperative |
| jekk | whether; if | Introduces an indirect question here |
| tistgħux | whether you can | `tistgħu` “you (plural) can” + marker `-x` |
| tattendu | you attend | Second-person plural form |
| sal-1 | by the 1st; until the 1st | Contracted `sa` + `l-` |
| ta' | of | Date linker |
| Lulju | July | Month name |

### 6.

**Malti:** Nistennew bil-ħerqa li niċċelebraw dan il-jum speċjali magħkom.

**Translation:** We look forward to celebrating this special day with you.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nistennew | we wait; we look forward | First-person plural form |
| bil-ħerqa | eagerly; with anticipation | Fixed adverbial phrase |
| li | to; introduces a clause | Conjunction |
| niċċelebraw | we celebrate | First-person plural form |
| dan | this | Masculine singular demonstrative, agreeing with `jum` |
| il-jum | the day | Masculine noun with the definite article |
| speċjali | special | Adjective |
| magħkom | with you (plural) | `ma'` “with” + `-kom` “you” |

**Malti:** Dejjem tagħkom, Anna u Mark

**Translation:** Yours always, Anna and Mark.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dejjem | always; forever | Adverb |
| tagħkom | yours | Second-person plural possessive, used as an email closing |
| Anna | Anna | Name |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| Mark | Mark | Name |
