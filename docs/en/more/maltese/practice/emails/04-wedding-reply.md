# Reply to a Wedding Invitation

**Scenario:** A guest replies to the couple and confirms that he and his wife will attend the wedding.

## Full Email

> Għeżież Anna u Mark,
>
> Grazzi tal-istedina għat-tieġ tagħkom.
>
> Ninsabu kuntenti li nikkonfermaw li se nattendu.
>
> Jien u marti se niġu għaċ-ċerimonja u għar-riċeviment.
>
> Naslu fil-ħin.
>
> Għandna nġibu xi ħaġa għalikom?
>
> Nawguralkom ħafna hena u mħabba fil-ħajja miżżewġa tagħkom.
>
> Nistennew bil-ħerqa li niċċelebraw magħkom.
>
> Grazzi mill-ġdid. Dejjem tagħkom, David

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

**Malti:** Għeżież Anna u Mark,

**Translation:** Dear Anna and Mark,

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għeżież | dear | Masculine plural form of address |
| Anna | Anna | Name |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| Mark | Mark | Name |

### 1.

**Malti:** Grazzi tal-istedina għat-tieġ tagħkom.

**Translation:** Thank you for inviting us to your wedding.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Grazzi | thank you | Polite expression |
| tal-istedina | for the invitation; of the invitation | Contracted `ta' + l-istedina` |
| għat-tieġ | to the wedding | Contracted `għal` + `it-tieġ` |
| tagħkom | your; yours | Second-person plural possessive |

### 2.

**Malti:** Ninsabu kuntenti li nikkonfermaw li se nattendu.

**Translation:** We are happy to confirm that we will attend.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ninsabu | we are; we find ourselves | First-person plural form |
| kuntenti | happy | Plural form |
| li | that; introduces a clause | Conjunction |
| nikkonfermaw | we confirm | First-person plural form |
| li | that; introduces a clause | Conjunction |
| se | will | Future marker |
| nattendu | we attend | First-person plural form |

### 3.

**Malti:** Jien u marti se niġu għaċ-ċerimonja u għar-riċeviment.

**Translation:** My wife and I will come to the ceremony and the reception.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jien | I | Emphatic pronoun |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| marti | my wife | `mara` “woman/wife” with a first-person possessive form |
| se | will | Future marker |
| niġu | we come | First-person plural form |
| għaċ-ċerimonja | to the ceremony | Contracted `għal` + `iċ-ċerimonja` |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| għar-riċeviment | to the reception | Contracted `għal` + `ir-riċeviment` |

### 4.

**Malti:** Naslu fil-ħin.

**Translation:** We will arrive on time.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Naslu | we arrive | First-person plural form; expresses a future arrangement here |
| fil-ħin | on time; in time | Contracted `f'` + `il-ħin` |

### 5.

**Malti:** Għandna nġibu xi ħaġa għalikom?

**Translation:** Should we bring anything for you?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għandna | we have; should we | First-person plural form; with a following verb can express obligation/need |
| nġibu | we bring | First-person plural form |
| xi | some; anything | Means “anything” in this question |
| ħaġa | thing | Feminine noun |
| għalikom | for you (plural) | `għal` “for” + `-kom` “you” |

### 6.

**Malti:** Nawguralkom ħafna hena u mħabba fil-ħajja miżżewġa tagħkom.

**Translation:** I wish you much happiness and love in your married life.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nawguralkom | I wish you | `nawgura` “I wish” + `-lkom` “you (plural)” |
| ħafna | much; a lot | Quantifier/adverb of degree |
| hena | happiness; joy | Masculine noun |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| mħabba | love | Feminine noun |
| fil-ħajja | in life | Contracted `f'` + `il-ħajja` |
| miżżewġa | married | Feminine form agreeing with `ħajja` |
| tagħkom | your; yours | Second-person plural possessive |

### 7.

**Malti:** Nistennew bil-ħerqa li niċċelebraw magħkom.

**Translation:** We look forward to celebrating with you.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nistennew | we wait; we look forward | First-person plural form |
| bil-ħerqa | eagerly; with anticipation | Fixed adverbial phrase |
| li | to; introduces a clause | Conjunction |
| niċċelebraw | we celebrate | First-person plural form |
| magħkom | with you (plural) | `ma'` “with” + `-kom` “you” |

**Malti:** Grazzi mill-ġdid. Dejjem tagħkom, David

**Translation:** Thanks again. Yours always, David.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Grazzi | thank you; thanks | Polite expression |
| mill-ġdid | again; once more | Fixed contracted expression from `minn` + `il-ġdid` |
| Dejjem | always; forever | Adverb |
| tagħkom | yours | Email closing |
| David | David | Name |
