# Dinner Invitation

**Scenario:** Anna invites her friend Mark to dinner at her home.

## Full Email

> Għażiż Mark,
>
> Nispera li inti tajjeb.
>
> Nixtieq nistednek għall-ikla ta' filgħaxija d-dar tiegħi nhar il-Ġimgħa.
>
> Se nibdew fis-sebgħa ta' filgħaxija.
>
> Se nkunu ftit ħbieb u nieklu flimkien.
>
> Se nipprepara platt Malti u deżerta.
>
> Jekk jogħġbok, għidli jekk għandek xi preferenzi għall-ikel.
>
> Nispera li tkun tista' tiġi.
>
> Tiegħek, Anna

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

**Malti:** Għażiż Mark,

**Translation:** Dear Mark,

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għażiż | dear | Masculine singular form of address |
| Mark | Mark | Name |

### 1.

**Malti:** Nispera li inti tajjeb.

**Translation:** I hope you are well.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nispera | I hope | First-person singular form |
| li | that; introduces a clause | Conjunction |
| inti | you | Second-person singular pronoun |
| tajjeb | well; good | Masculine form; feminine form is `tajba` |

### 2.

**Malti:** Nixtieq nistednek għall-ikla ta' filgħaxija d-dar tiegħi nhar il-Ġimgħa.

**Translation:** I would like to invite you to dinner at my home on Friday evening.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nixtieq | I would like; I wish | Polite expression of a wish |
| nistednek | I invite you | First-person form of the invitation verb with a second-person singular object suffix |
| għall-ikla | to the meal; for dinner | `għal` + definite article `l-` contracts with `ikla` |
| ta' | of; during | Links the noun and time expression |
| filgħaxija | in the evening | Time expression |
| d-dar | at home; the house | Common contracted spelling of `id-dar` |
| tiegħi | my; mine | First-person singular possessive |
| nhar | on the day of | Introduces a weekday or date |
| il-Ġimgħa | Friday | Weekday commonly used with the definite article |

### 3.

**Malti:** Se nibdew fis-sebgħa ta' filgħaxija.

**Translation:** We will start at seven in the evening.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Se | will; going to | Future marker |
| nibdew | we start | First-person plural form |
| fis-sebgħa | at seven | `f'` contracts with the definite clock-hour form |
| ta' | of; during | Links the hour and time of day |
| filgħaxija | in the evening | Time expression |

### 4.

**Malti:** Se nkunu ftit ħbieb u nieklu flimkien.

**Translation:** A few friends will be there, and we will eat together.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Se | will | Future marker |
| nkunu | we will be | First-person plural form of `ikun` |
| ftit | a few; a little | Quantifier |
| ħbieb | friends | Plural of `ħabib` |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| nieklu | we eat | First-person plural form |
| flimkien | together | Adverb |

### 5.

**Malti:** Se nipprepara platt Malti u deżerta.

**Translation:** I will prepare a Maltese dish and dessert.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Se | will | Future marker |
| nipprepara | I prepare | First-person singular form |
| platt | dish; plate | Masculine noun; means “dish” here |
| Malti | Maltese-style | Masculine singular form agreeing with `platt` |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| deżerta | dessert | Feminine noun |

### 6.

**Malti:** Jekk jogħġbok, għidli jekk għandek xi preferenzi għall-ikel.

**Translation:** Please tell me if you have any food preferences.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jekk jogħġbok | please | Fixed polite expression |
| għidli | tell me | `għid` “tell” + `-li` “me” |
| jekk | whether; if | Introduces an indirect question here |
| għandek | you have | Second-person singular form of `għand` |
| xi | some; any | Indefinite determiner |
| preferenzi | preferences | Plural of `preferenza` |
| għall-ikel | for food; regarding food | `għal` contracts with the definite article before `ikel` |

### 7.

**Malti:** Nispera li tkun tista' tiġi.

**Translation:** I hope you will be able to come.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nispera | I hope | First-person singular form |
| li | that; introduces a clause | Conjunction |
| tkun | you will be; you may be | Second-person/subordinate form of `ikun` |
| tista' | you can | Second-person singular form |
| tiġi | you come | Second-person singular form |

**Malti:** Tiegħek, Anna

**Translation:** Yours, Anna.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tiegħek | yours; sincerely | Email closing |
| Anna | Anna | Name |
