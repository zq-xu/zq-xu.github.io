# Reply to a Dinner Invitation

**Scenario:** Mark accepts Anna's dinner invitation and offers to bring dessert.

## Email Header

**Lil (To):** anna.c@gmail.com
**Mingħand (From):** mark.b@gmail.com
**Data (Date):** 14 ta' Ottubru 2026
**Suġġett (Subject):** Risposta għall-ikla ta' filgħaxija

## Full Email

> **Lil:** anna.c@gmail.com
> **Mingħand:** mark.b@gmail.com
> **Data:** 14 ta' Ottubru 2026
> **Suġġett:** Risposta għall-ikla ta' filgħaxija
>
> Għażiża Anna,
>
> Grazzi tal-istedina għall-ikla ta' filgħaxija.
>
> Iva, se niġi nhar il-Ġimgħa.
>
> Se nasal fis-sebgħa.
>
> M'għandi l-ebda preferenza għall-ikel.
>
> Nista' nġib xi ħaġa għad-deżerta?
>
> Se nġib kejk żgħir.
>
> Nistenna bil-ħerqa li niltaqa' mal-ħbieb tiegħek.
>

> Mark

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

**Malti:** Lil: anna.c@gmail.com

**Translation:** To: anna.c@gmail.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lil | to; recipient | Maltese label for the email "To" field |
| anna.c@gmail.com | email address | Recipient's (Anna's) email |

**Malti:** Mingħand: mark.b@gmail.com

**Translation:** From: mark.b@gmail.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mingħand | from; sender | Maltese label for the email "From" field; literally "from ..." |
| mark.b@gmail.com | email address | Sender's (Mark's) email |

**Malti:** Data: 14 ta' Ottubru 2026

**Translation:** Date: 14 October 2026

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Data | date | Email "Date" field; Maltese date format is "day ta' month year" |
| 14 | fourteen | Day in the date |
| ta' | of | Links the day and the month |
| Ottubru | October | Month name |

**Malti:** Suġġett: Risposta għall-ikla ta' filgħaxija

**Translation:** Subject: Reply to the Dinner Invitation

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Suġġett | subject | Email "Subject" field |
| Risposta | reply; answer | Feminine noun |
| għall-ikla | to the meal | Contracted `għal` + `l-ikla` |
| ta' | of; during | Links the noun and time expression |
| filgħaxija | in the evening | Time expression |

**Malti:** Għażiża Anna,

**Translation:** Dear Anna,

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għażiża | dear | Feminine singular form of address |
| Anna | Anna | Name |

### 1.

**Malti:** Grazzi tal-istedina għall-ikla ta' filgħaxija.

**Translation:** Thank you for inviting me to dinner.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Grazzi | thank you | Polite expression |
| tal-istedina | for the invitation; of the invitation | Contracted `ta'` + definite article |
| għall-ikla | to the meal; for dinner | Contracted `għal` + definite article |
| ta' | of; during | Links the noun and time expression |
| filgħaxija | in the evening | Time expression |

### 2.

**Malti:** Iva, se niġi nhar il-Ġimgħa.

**Translation:** Yes, I will come on Friday.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Iva | yes; all right | Affirmative response |
| se | will | Future marker |
| niġi | I come | First-person singular form |
| nhar | on the day of | Introduces a weekday or date |
| il-Ġimgħa | Friday | Weekday commonly used with the definite article |

### 3.

**Malti:** Se nasal fis-sebgħa.

**Translation:** I will arrive at seven.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Se | will | Future marker |
| nasal | I arrive | First-person singular form |
| fis-sebgħa | at seven | `f'` contracts with the definite clock-hour form |

### 4.

**Malti:** M'għandi l-ebda preferenza għall-ikel.

**Translation:** I don't have any food preferences.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| M'għandi | I do not have | One contracted/negative form of `ma għandix` |
| l-ebda | no; not any | Negative determiner |
| preferenza | preference | Feminine noun |
| għall-ikel | for food; regarding food | `għal` contracts with the definite article |

### 5.

**Malti:** Nista' nġib xi ħaġa għad-deżerta?

**Translation:** Can I bring something for dessert?

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nista' | can I? | First-person singular form |
| nġib | I bring | First-person singular form |
| xi | some; a certain | Indefinite determiner |
| ħaġa | thing | Feminine noun |
| għad-deżerta | for dessert | `għal` contracts with the definite article |

### 6.

**Malti:** Se nġib kejk żgħir.

**Translation:** I will bring a small cake.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Se | will | Future marker |
| nġib | I bring | First-person singular form |
| kejk | cake | Masculine noun |
| żgħir | small | Masculine singular form agreeing with `kejk` |

### 7.

**Malti:** Nistenna bil-ħerqa li niltaqa' mal-ħbieb tiegħek.

**Translation:** I look forward to meeting your friends.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nistenna | I wait; I look forward | First-person singular form |
| bil-ħerqa | eagerly; with anticipation | Fixed adverbial phrase |
| li | to; introduces a clause | Conjunction |
| niltaqa' | I meet | First-person singular form |
| mal-ħbieb | with the friends | `ma'` contracts with the definite article |
| tiegħek | your; yours | Second-person singular possessive |

**Malti:** Tislijiet, Mark

**Translation:** Regards, Mark (email closing).

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tislijiet | regards; best wishes | Formal email/letter closing, literally "greetings/regards" |
| Mark | Mark | Name |
