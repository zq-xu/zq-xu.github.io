# Chef Job Application

**Scenario:** The applicant writes to the restaurant manager to apply for the position of chef.

## Email Header

**Lil (To):** info@restorant.com
**Mingħand (From):** [isem]@email.com
**Data (Date):** 15 ta' Ottubru 2026
**Suġġett (Subject):** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' kok

## Full Email

> **Lil:** info@restorant.com
> **Mingħand:** [isem]@email.com
> **Data:** 15 ta' Ottubru 2026
> **Suġġett:** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' kok
>
> Għażiż Sinjur / Sinjura,
>
> Qed nikteb biex napplika għall-impjieg ta' kok fir-ristorant tagħkom.
>
> Għandi ħames snin esperjenza fil-kċina.
>
> Naf insajjar platti tradizzjonali Maltin u Mediterranji.
>
> Jien bniedem li jaħdem iebes u jimpurtani mill-kwalità tal-ikel.
>
> Naħdem ukoll tajjeb ma' tim.
>
> Nixtieq niltaqgħa miegħkom għal intervista meta jkun konvenjenti.
>
> Grazzi ħafna talli qiesu l-applikazzjoni tiegħi.
>
> Nispera li nisma' mingħandkom dalwaqt.
>
> Tislijiet,
> [Ismek]

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

**Malti:** Lil: info@restorant.com

**Translation:** To: info@restorant.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lil | to; recipient | Maltese label for the email "To" field |
| info@restorant.com | email address | The restaurant's recruitment email (example) |

**Malti:** Mingħand: [isem]@email.com

**Translation:** From: [your name]@email.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mingħand | from; sender | Maltese label for the email "From" field; literally "from ..." |
| [isem]@email.com | email address | Placeholder for your email; replace with your real one |

**Malti:** Data: 15 ta' Ottubru 2026

**Translation:** Date: 15 October 2026

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Data | date | Email "Date" field; Maltese date format is "day ta' month year" |
| 15 | fifteen | Day in the date |
| ta' | of | Links the day and the month |
| Ottubru | October | Month name |

**Malti:** Suġġett: Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' kok

**Translation:** Subject: Application for the Position of Chef

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Suġġett | subject | Email "Subject" field |
| Applikazzjoni | application | Feminine loanword from application |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| xogħol | work; job | Masculine noun |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| kok | chef; cook | Masculine loanword from cook |

**Malti:** Għażiż Sinjur / Sinjura,

**Translation:** Dear Sir / Madam,

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għażiż | dear | Masculine singular form |
| Sinjur | sir; Mr. | Masculine noun |
| / | or | Indicates a choice between two |
| Sinjura | madam; Mrs. | Feminine noun |

### 1.

**Malti:** Qed nikteb biex napplika għall-impjieg ta' kok fir-ristorant tagħkom.

**Translation:** I am writing to apply for the position of chef in your restaurant.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Qed | am (progressive marker) | Used with a verb to indicate an ongoing action |
| nikteb | I write | First-person singular present |
| biex | in order to | Introduces a purpose clause |
| napplika | I apply | First-person singular form, loanword from apply |
| għall-impjieg | for the job | Contracted `għal` + `l-impjieg` |
| ta' | of | Possession/category linker |
| kok | chef; cook | Masculine loanword from cook |
| fir-ristorant | in the restaurant | Contracted `f'` + `ir-ristorant` |
| tagħkom | your (plural) | Plural possessive pronoun |

### 2.

**Malti:** Għandi ħames snin esperjenza fil-kċina.

**Translation:** I have five years of kitchen experience.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għandi | I have | First-person singular form of `għand` |
| ħames | five | Numeral followed by a plural noun |
| snin | years | Plural of `sena` |
| esperjenza | experience | Feminine noun |
| fil-kċina | in the kitchen | Contracted `f'` + `il-kċina` |

### 3.

**Malti:** Naf insajjar platti tradizzjonali Maltin u Mediterranji.

**Translation:** I can cook traditional Maltese and Mediterranean dishes.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Naf | I know; I can | First-person singular form |
| insajjar | I cook | First-person singular present |
| platti | dishes | Plural of `platt` |
| tradizzjonali | traditional | Adjective |
| Maltin | Maltese | Plural of `Malti` |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| Mediterranji | Mediterranean | Plural adjective |

### 4.

**Malti:** Jien bniedem li jaħdem iebes u jimpurtani mill-kwalità tal-ikel.

**Translation:** I am a person who works hard and cares about the quality of the food.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jien | I | First-person singular pronoun |
| bniedem | person; human being | Masculine noun |
| li | who; that | Relative pronoun |
| jaħdem | he works | Third-person masculine singular |
| iebes | hard; industrious | Used as an adverb "hard" here |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| jimpurtani | I care | `jimpurta` + `-ni` "me"; idiomatic expression |
| mill-kwalità | about the quality | Contracted `minn` + `il-kwalità` |
| tal-ikel | of the food | Contracted `ta'` + `l-ikel` |

### 5.

**Malti:** Naħdem ukoll tajjeb ma' tim.

**Translation:** I also work well with a team.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Naħdem | I work | First-person singular present |
| ukoll | also; as well | Adverb |
| tajjeb | well | Used as an adverb here |
| ma' | with | Preposition |
| tim | team | Masculine loanword from team |

### 6.

**Malti:** Nixtieq niltaqgħa miegħkom għal intervista meta jkun konvenjenti.

**Translation:** I would like to meet you for an interview whenever it is convenient.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nixtieq | I would like; I wish | Polite expression of a wish |
| niltaqgħa | I meet | First-person singular form |
| miegħkom | with you (plural) | `miegħ` "with" + `-kom` "you" |
| għal | for | Preposition |
| intervista | interview | Feminine loanword from interview |
| meta | when | Conjunction |
| jkun | it will be | Third-person singular, used with `konvenjenti` |
| konvenjenti | convenient | Adjective |

### 7.

**Malti:** Grazzi ħafna talli qiesu l-applikazzjoni tiegħi.

**Translation:** Thank you very much for considering my application.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Grazzi | thank you | Polite phrase |
| ħafna | very; much | Adverb of degree |
| talli | for (your) ... | Written expression introducing a reason |
| qiesu | they considered | Third-person plural past of `qies` "consider/look at" |
| l-applikazzjoni | the application | Feminine noun with the definite article |
| tiegħi | my; mine | Possessive pronoun |

### 8.

**Malti:** Nispera li nisma' mingħandkom dalwaqt.

**Translation:** I hope to hear from you soon.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nispera | I hope | First-person singular form |
| li | that; introduces a clause | Conjunction |
| nisma' | I hear; I receive news | First-person singular form |
| mingħandkom | from you (plural) | `mingħand` "from ..." + `-kom` "you" |
| dalwaqt | soon | Adverb, from `dal-` "this" + `waqt` "moment" |

**Malti:** Tislijiet, [Ismek]

**Translation:** Regards, [your name] (formal email closing).

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tislijiet | regards; best wishes | Formal email/letter closing, literally "greetings/regards" |
| Ismek | your name | `isem` "name" + `-ek` "your"; replace with your real name |
