# Cafe Waiter Job Application

**Scenario:** The applicant writes to the cafe manager to apply for the position of waiter.

## Email Header

**Lil (To):** info@kafetterija.com
**Mingħand (From):** [isem]@email.com
**Data (Date):** 15 ta' Ottubru 2026
**Suġġett (Subject):** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' wejter

## Full Email

> **Lil:** info@kafetterija.com
> **Mingħand:** [isem]@email.com
> **Data:** 15 ta' Ottubru 2026
> **Suġġett:** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' wejter
>
> Għażiż Sinjur / Sinjura,
>
> Qed nikteb biex napplika għall-impjieg ta' wejter fil-kafetterija tagħkom.
>
> Għandi esperjenza ta' sentejn fis-servizz tal-klijenti.
>
> Nista' naqdi lill-klijenti b'mod edukat u bil-ħeffa.
>
> Nitgħallem malajr u nifhem il-bżonnijiet tal-klijenti.
>
> Jien persuna ta' ħbiberija u nħobb naħdem man-nies.
>
> Nista' naħdem filgħodu u filgħaxija.
>
> Nispera li ningħaqad mat-tim tagħkom.
>
> Grazzi talli ħadtu ħsieb l-applikazzjoni tiegħi.
>
> Nistenna bil-ħerqa li nisma' mingħandkom.
>
> Tislijiet,
> [Ismek]

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

**Malti:** Lil: info@kafetterija.com

**Translation:** To: info@kafetterija.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lil | to; recipient | Maltese label for the email "To" field |
| info@kafetterija.com | email address | The cafe's recruitment email (example) |

**Malti:** Mingħand: [isem]@email.com

**Translation:** From: [your name]@email.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mingħand | from; sender | Maltese label for the email "From" field; literally "from ..." |
| [isem]@email.com | email address | Placeholder for your email; replace with your real one |

**Malti:** Data: 15 ta' Ottubru 2026

**Translation:** Date: 15 October 2026

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Data | date | Email "Date" field; Maltese date format is "day ta' month year" |
| 15 | fifteen | Day in the date |
| ta' | of | Links the day and the month |
| Ottubru | October | Month name |

**Malti:** Suġġett: Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' wejter

**Translation:** Subject: Application for the Position of Waiter

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Suġġett | subject | Email "Subject" field |
| Applikazzjoni | application | Feminine loanword from application |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| xogħol | work; job | Masculine noun |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| wejter | waiter | Masculine loanword from waiter |

**Malti:** Għażiż Sinjur / Sinjura,

**Translation:** Dear Sir / Madam,

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għażiż | dear | Masculine singular form |

| Sinjura | madam; Mrs. | Feminine noun |

### 1.

**Malti:** Qed nikteb biex napplika għall-impjieg ta' wejter fil-kafetterija tagħkom.

**Translation:** I am writing to apply for the position of waiter in your cafe.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Qed | am (progressive marker) | Used with a verb to indicate an ongoing action |
| nikteb | I write | First-person singular present |
| biex | in order to | Introduces a purpose clause |
| napplika | I apply | First-person singular form, loanword from apply |
| għall-impjieg | for the job | Contracted `għal` + `l-impjieg` |
| ta' | of | Possession/category linker |
| wejter | waiter | Masculine loanword from waiter |
| fil-kafetterija | in the cafe | Contracted `f'` + `il-kafetterija` |
| tagħkom | your (plural) | Plural possessive pronoun |

### 2.

**Malti:** Għandi esperjenza ta' sentejn fis-servizz tal-klijenti.

**Translation:** I have two years of experience in customer service.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għandi | I have | First-person singular form of `għand` |
| esperjenza | experience | Feminine noun |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| sentejn | two years | Fixed form meaning "two years" |
| fis-servizz | in service | Contracted `f'` + `is-servizz` |
| tal-klijenti | of the customers | Contracted `ta'` + `il-klijenti` (plural) |

### 3.

**Malti:** Nista' naqdi lill-klijenti b'mod edukat u bil-ħeffa.

**Translation:** I can serve customers politely and quickly.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nista' | I can | First-person singular form |
| naqdi | I serve | First-person singular present |
| lill-klijenti | to the customers | Contracted `li` + `l-klijenti`, marks the object |
| b'mod | in a ... way | `b'` "with" + `mod` "manner" |
| edukat | polite; well-mannered | Adjective |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| bil-ħeffa | quickly | Contracted `b'` + `il-ħeffa` "speed" |

### 8.

**Malti:** Grazzi talli ħadtu ħsieb l-applikazzjoni tiegħi.

**Translation:** Thank you for considering my application.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Grazzi | thank you | Polite phrase |
| talli | for (your) ... | Written expression introducing a reason |
| ħadtu | you took | Third-person plural past of `ħa` "take" |
| ħsieb | consideration; mind | `ħadtu ħsieb` is a fixed phrase meaning "you considered" |
| l-applikazzjoni | the application | Feminine noun with the definite article |
| tiegħi | my; mine | Possessive pronoun |

### 9.

**Malti:** Nistenna bil-ħerqa li nisma' mingħandkom.

**Translation:** I eagerly look forward to hearing from you.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| li | to; introduces a clause | Conjunction |
| nisma' | I hear; receive news | First-person singular form |
| mingħandkom | from you (plural) | `mingħand` + `-kom` "you" |

**Translation:** Regards, [your name] (formal email closing).
