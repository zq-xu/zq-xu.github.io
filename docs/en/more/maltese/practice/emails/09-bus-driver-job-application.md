# Bus Driver Job Application

**Scenario:** The applicant writes to the bus company to apply for the position of bus driver.

## Email Header

**Lil (To):** info@transport.com
**Mingħand (From):** [isem]@email.com
**Data (Date):** 15 ta' Ottubru 2026
**Suġġett (Subject):** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' sewwieq tal-karozza tal-linja

> **Lil:** info@transport.com
> **Mingħand:** [isem]@email.com

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

**Malti:** Lil: info@transport.com

**Translation:** To: info@transport.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lil | to; recipient | Maltese label for the email "To" field |
| info@transport.com | email address | The bus company's recruitment email (example) |

**Malti:** Mingħand: [isem]@email.com

**Translation:** From: [your name]@email.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mingħand | from; sender | Maltese label for the email "From" field; literally "from ..." |
| [isem]@email.com | email address | Placeholder for your email; replace with your real one |

**Malti:** Data: 15 ta' Ottubru 2026

**Translation:** Date: 15 October 2026

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Data | date | Email "Date" field; Maltese date format is "day ta' month year" |
| 15 | fifteen | Day in the date |
| ta' | of | Links the day and the month |
| Ottubru | October | Month name |

**Malti:** Suġġett: Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' sewwieq tal-karozza tal-linja

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |

### 1.

**Malti:** Qed nikteb biex napplika għall-impjieg ta' sewwieq tal-karozza tal-linja.

**Translation:** I am writing to apply for the position of bus driver.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Qed | am (progressive marker) | Used with a verb to indicate an ongoing action |
| nikteb | I write | First-person singular present |
| biex | in order to | Introduces a purpose clause |
| napplika | I apply | First-person singular form, loanword from apply |
| għall-impjieg | for the job | Contracted `għal` + `l-impjieg` |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| sewwieq | driver | Masculine noun |
| tal-karozza | of the car | Contracted `ta'` + `il-karozza` |
| tal-linja | of the line | Contracted `ta'` + `il-linja`; together "the bus" |

### 2.

**Malti:** Għandi liċenzja tas-sewqan tal-kategorija D.

**Translation:** I hold a category D driving licence.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għandi | I have | First-person singular form of `għand` |
| liċenzja | licence | Feminine loanword from licence |
| tas-sewqan | of driving | Contracted `ta'` + `is-sewqan` |
| tal-kategorija | of the category | Contracted `ta'` + `il-kategorija` |
| D | D (category) | Refers to the large-vehicle driving category |

### 3.

**Malti:** Għandi esperjenza ta' tliet snin bħala sewwieq tal-karozza tal-linja.

**Translation:** I have three years of experience as a bus driver.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għandi | I have | First-person singular form of `għand` |
| esperjenza | experience | Feminine noun |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| tliet | three | Numeral followed by a plural noun |
| snin | years | Plural of `sena` |
| bħala | as | Preposition |
| sewwieq | driver | Masculine noun |
| tal-karozza tal-linja | of the bus | As above, "the line's car" |

### 4.

**Malti:** Naf it-toroq ta' Malta tajjeb ħafna.

**Translation:** I know the roads of Malta very well.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Naf | I know; I am familiar with | First-person singular form |
| it-toroq | the roads | Plural of `triq`, with the definite article |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |

### 5.

**Malti:** Jien responsabbli u jimpurtani mis-sigurtà tal-passiġġieri.

**Translation:** I am responsible and I care about the safety of passengers.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jien | I | First-person singular pronoun |

### 7.

**Malti:** Nirrispetta r-regoli tat-triq u nżomm il-ħin.

**Translation:** I respect the traffic rules and I am punctual.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nirrispetta | I respect; I obey | First-person singular form, loanword from respect |

| il-ħin | the time | `nżomm il-ħin` is a fixed phrase meaning "to be punctual" |

**Translation:** Thank you for considering my application.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |

| tiegħi | my; mine | Possessive pronoun |
