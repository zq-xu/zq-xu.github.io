# Taxi Driver Job Application

**Scenario:** The applicant writes to the taxi company to apply for the position of taxi driver.

## Email Header

**Lil (To):** info@taksis.com
**Mingħand (From):** [isem]@email.com
**Data (Date):** 15 ta' Ottubru 2026
**Suġġett (Subject):** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' sewwieq tat-taksi

## Full Email

> **Lil:** info@taksis.com
> **Mingħand:** [isem]@email.com
> **Data:** 15 ta' Ottubru 2026
> **Suġġett:** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' sewwieq tat-taksi
>
> Għażiż Sinjur / Sinjura,

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

**Malti:** Lil: info@taksis.com

**Translation:** To: info@taksis.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lil | to; recipient | Maltese label for the email "To" field |
| info@taksis.com | email address | The taxi company's recruitment email (example) |

**Malti:** Mingħand: [isem]@email.com

**Translation:** From: [your name]@email.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mingħand | from; sender | Maltese label for the email "From" field; literally "from ..." |
| [isem]@email.com | email address | Placeholder for your email; replace with your real one |

**Malti:** Data: 15 ta' Ottubru 2026

**Translation:** Date: 15 October 2026

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Data | date | Email "Date" field; Maltese date format is "day ta' month year" |
| 15 | fifteen | Day in the date |
| ta' | of | Links the day and the month |
| Ottubru | October | Month name |

**Malti:** Suġġett: Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' sewwieq tat-taksi

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |

### 1.

**Malti:** Qed nikteb biex napplika għall-impjieg ta' sewwieq tat-taksi.

**Translation:** I am writing to apply for the position of taxi driver.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Qed | am (progressive marker) | Used with a verb to indicate an ongoing action |
| nikteb | I write | First-person singular present |
| biex | in order to | Introduces a purpose clause |
| napplika | I apply | First-person singular form, loanword from apply |
| għall-impjieg | for the job | Contracted `għal` + `l-impjieg` |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| sewwieq | driver | Masculine noun |
| tat-taksi | of the taxi | Contracted `ta'` + `it-taksi` |

### 2.

**Translation:** I hold a driving licence and a special taxi licence.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għandi | I have | First-person singular form of `għand` |
| liċenzja | licence | Feminine loanword from licence |
| tas-sewqan | of driving | Contracted `ta'` + `is-sewqan` |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| liċenzja | licence | Feminine noun |
| speċjali | special | Adjective loanword from special |
| tat-taksi | of the taxi | Contracted `ta'` + `it-taksi` |

### 3.

**Malti:** Naf it-toroq ta' Malta u ta' Għawdex tajjeb ħafna.

**Translation:** I know the roads of Malta and Gozo very well.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Naf | I know; I am familiar with | First-person singular form |
| it-toroq | the roads | Plural of `triq`, with the definite article |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| Malta | Malta | Place name |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| Għawdex | Gozo | Malta's second-largest island |
| tajjeb | well | Used as an adverb here |
| ħafna | very | Adverb of degree |

### 5.

### 6.

**Malti:** Nista' naħdem billejl u fi tmiem il-ġimgħa.

**Translation:** I can work at night and at weekends.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nista' | I can | First-person singular form |

| billejl | at night | Contracted form of `bil-lejl` "in the evening/night" |
