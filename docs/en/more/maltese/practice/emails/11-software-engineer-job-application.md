# Software Engineer Job Application

**Scenario:** The applicant writes to the tech company to apply for the position of software engineer.

## Email Header

**Lil (To):** info@tech.com
**Mingħand (From):** [isem]@email.com
**Data (Date):** 15 ta' Ottubru 2026
**Suġġett (Subject):** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' inġinier tas-softwer

## Full Email

> **Lil:** info@tech.com
> **Mingħand:** [isem]@email.com
> **Data:** 15 ta' Ottubru 2026
> **Suġġett:** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' inġinier tas-softwer
>
> Għażiż Sinjur / Sinjura,
>
> Qed nikteb biex napplika għall-pożizzjoni ta' inġinier tas-softwer.
>
> Għandi lawrja fil-kompjuter u esperjenza ta' ħames snin fl-iżvilupp tas-softwer.
>
> Naf il-lingwi tal-programmar bħal Java, Python u JavaScript.
>
> Naħdem fuq proġetti bħal apps u websajts.
>
> Jien kreattiv u nsolvi l-problemi b'mod effiċjenti.
>
> Naħdem tajjeb f'tim u wkoll waħdi.
>
> Jimpurtani mill-kwalità u mid-dettalji.
>
> Nixtieq inkun parti mit-tim tagħkom.
>
> Nispera li niltaqgħu għal intervista dalwaqt.
>
> Grazzi talli qiesu l-applikazzjoni tiegħi.
>
> Tislijiet,
> [Ismek]

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

**Malti:** Lil: info@tech.com

**Translation:** To: info@tech.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lil | to; recipient | Maltese label for the email "To" field |
| info@tech.com | email address | The tech company's recruitment email (example) |

**Malti:** Mingħand: [isem]@email.com

**Translation:** From: [your name]@email.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mingħand | from; sender | Maltese label for the email "From" field; literally "from ..." |
| [isem]@email.com | email address | Placeholder for your email; replace with your real one |

**Malti:** Data: 15 ta' Ottubru 2026

**Translation:** Date: 15 October 2026

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Data | date | Email "Date" field; Maltese date format is "day ta' month year" |
| 15 | fifteen | Day in the date |
| ta' | of | Links the day and the month |

### 1.

**Malti:** Qed nikteb biex napplika għall-pożizzjoni ta' inġinier tas-softwer.

**Translation:** I am writing to apply for the position of software engineer.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Qed | am (progressive marker) | Used with a verb to indicate an ongoing action |
| nikteb | I write | First-person singular present |
| biex | in order to | Introduces a purpose clause |
| napplika | I apply | First-person singular form, loanword from apply |
| għall-pożizzjoni | for the position | Contracted `għal` + `il-pożizzjoni` |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| inġinier | engineer | Masculine loanword from engineer |
| tas-softwer | of the software | Contracted `ta'` + `is-softwer` |
