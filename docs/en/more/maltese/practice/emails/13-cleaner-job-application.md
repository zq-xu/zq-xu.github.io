# Cleaner Job Application

**Scenario:** The applicant writes to the cleaning/property company to apply for the position of cleaner.

## Email Header

**Lil (To):** info@tindif.com
**Mingħand (From):** [isem]@email.com
**Data (Date):** 15 ta' Ottubru 2026
**Suġġett (Subject):** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' haddiem tat-tindif

## Full Email

> **Lil:** info@tindif.com
> **Mingħand:** [isem]@email.com
> **Data:** 15 ta' Ottubru 2026
> **Suġġett:** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' haddiem tat-tindif
>
> Għażiż Sinjur / Sinjura,
>
> Qed nikteb biex napplika għall-impjieg ta' haddiem tat-tindif.
>
> Għandi esperjenza ta' sentejn fix-xogħol tat-tindif.
>
> Naf naddaf djar, uffiċini u bini ieħor.
>
> Jien bniedem li jaħdem iebes, onest u puntwali.
>
> Nista' naħdem filgħodu u filgħaxija, ukoll fi tmiem il-ġimgħa.
>
> Jimpurtani li nżomm kollox nadif u ordnat.
>
> Nispera li nisma' mingħandkom dalwaqt.
>
> Grazzi talli qiesu l-applikazzjoni tiegħi.
>
> Tislijiet,
> [Ismek]

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

**Malti:** Lil: info@tindif.com

**Translation:** To: info@tindif.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lil | to; recipient | Maltese label for the email "To" field |
| info@tindif.com | email address | The cleaning company's recruitment email (example) |

**Malti:** Mingħand: [isem]@email.com

**Translation:** From: [your name]@email.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mingħand | from; sender | Maltese label for the email "From" field; literally "from ..." |
| [isem]@email.com | email address | Placeholder for your email; replace with your real one |

**Malti:** Data: 15 ta' Ottubru 2026

**Translation:** Date: 15 October 2026

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Data | date | Email "Date" field; Maltese date format is "day ta' month year" |
| 15 | fifteen | Day in the date |
| ta' | of | Links the day and the month |
| Ottubru | October | Month name |

**Malti:** Suġġett: Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' haddiem tat-tindif

**Translation:** Subject: Application for the Position of Cleaner

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Suġġett | subject | Email "Subject" field |
| Applikazzjoni | application | Feminine loanword from application |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| xogħol | work; job | Masculine noun |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| haddiem | worker | Masculine noun |
| tat-tindif | of cleaning | Contracted `ta'` + `it-tindif` |

**Malti:** Għażiż Sinjur / Sinjura,

**Translation:** Dear Sir / Madam,

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għażiż | dear | Masculine singular form |

| Sinjura | madam; Mrs. | Feminine noun |

### 1.

**Malti:** Qed nikteb biex napplika għall-impjieg ta' haddiem tat-tindif.

**Translation:** I am writing to apply for the position of cleaner.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Qed | am (progressive marker) | Used with a verb to indicate an ongoing action |
| nikteb | I write | First-person singular present |
| biex | in order to | Introduces a purpose clause |
| napplika | I apply | First-person singular form, loanword from apply |
| għall-impjieg | for the job | Contracted `għal` + `l-impjieg` |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| haddiem | worker | Masculine noun |
| tat-tindif | of cleaning | Contracted `ta'` + `it-tindif` |

### 2.

**Malti:** Għandi esperjenza ta' sentejn fix-xogħol tat-tindif.

**Translation:** I have two years of experience in cleaning work.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għandi | I have | First-person singular form of `għand` |
| esperjenza | experience | Feminine noun |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| sentejn | two years | Fixed form meaning "two years" |
| fix-xogħol | in the work | Contracted `f'` + `ix-xogħol` |
| tat-tindif | of cleaning | Contracted `ta'` + `it-tindif` |

### 3.

**Translation:** I can clean houses, offices, and other buildings.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Naf | I know; I can | First-person singular form |
| naddaf | I clean | First-person singular present |
| djar | houses | Plural of `dar` |
| uffiċini | offices | Plural of `uffiċċju` |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| bini | buildings | Collective noun |
| ieħor | other | Masculine singular form |

### 4.

**Malti:** Jien bniedem li jaħdem iebes, onest u puntwali.

**Translation:** I am a hard-working, honest, and punctual person.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jien | I | First-person singular pronoun |
| bniedem | person; human being | Masculine noun |
| li | who; that | Relative pronoun |
| jaħdem | he works | Third-person masculine singular |
| iebes | hard; industrious | Used as an adverb "hard" here |
| onest | honest | Adjective loanword from honest |

| puntwali | punctual | Adjective loanword from punctual |

### 5.

**Malti:** Nista' naħdem filgħodu u filgħaxija, ukoll fi tmiem il-ġimgħa.

**Translation:** I can work in the morning and in the evening, also at weekends.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nista' | I can | First-person singular form |
| naħdem | I work | First-person singular present |
| filgħodu | in the morning | Time expression |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| filgħaxija | in the evening | Time expression |
| ukoll | also | Adverb |
| fi | in | Variant of the preposition `f'` |
| tmiem | end; close | `tmiem il-ġimgħa` is a fixed phrase meaning "weekend" |
| il-ġimgħa | the week | Feminine noun with the definite article |

**Translation:** Thank you for considering my application.
