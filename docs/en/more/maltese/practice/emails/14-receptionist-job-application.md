# Receptionist Job Application

**Scenario:** The applicant writes to a hotel (also usable for a bank) to apply for the position of receptionist.

## Email Header

**Lil (To):** info@lukanda.com
**Mingħand (From):** [isem]@email.com
**Data (Date):** 15 ta' Ottubru 2026
**Suġġett (Subject):** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' receptionist

## Full Email

> **Lil:** info@lukanda.com
> **Mingħand:** [isem]@email.com
> **Data:** 15 ta' Ottubru 2026
> **Suġġett:** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' receptionist
>
> Għażiż Sinjur / Sinjura,
>
> Qed nikteb biex napplika għall-impjieg ta' receptionist.
>
> Għandi esperjenza ta' tliet snin fis-servizz tal-klijenti.
>
> Naf nitkellem Malti, Ingliż u ftit Taljan.
>
> Nista' nwieġeb it-telefon u nilqa' lill-klijenti b'mod edukat.
>
> Jien nadif, puntwali u responsabbli.
>
> Nista' naħdem billejl u fi tmiem il-ġimgħa.
>
> Naf nuża l-kompjuter u nittratta mal-flus b'attenzjoni.
>
> Nispera li nisma' mingħandkom dalwaqt.
>
> Grazzi talli qiesu l-applikazzjoni tiegħi.
>
> Tislijiet,
> [Ismek]

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

**Malti:** Lil: info@lukanda.com

**Translation:** To: info@lukanda.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lil | to; recipient | Maltese label for the email "To" field |
| info@lukanda.com | email address | The hotel's recruitment email (example) |

**Malti:** Mingħand: [isem]@email.com

**Translation:** From: [your name]@email.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mingħand | from; sender | Maltese label for the email "From" field; literally "from ..." |
| [isem]@email.com | email address | Placeholder for your email; replace with your real one |

**Malti:** Data: 15 ta' Ottubru 2026

**Translation:** Date: 15 October 2026

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Data | date | Email "Date" field; Maltese date format is "day ta' month year" |
| 15 | fifteen | Day in the date |
| ta' | of | Links the day and the month |
| Ottubru | October | Month name |

**Malti:** Suġġett: Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' receptionist

**Translation:** Subject: Application for the Position of Receptionist

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Suġġett | subject | Email "Subject" field |
| Applikazzjoni | application | Feminine loanword from application |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| xogħol | work; job | Masculine noun |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| receptionist | receptionist; front-desk clerk | Loanword from receptionist |

**Malti:** Għażiż Sinjur / Sinjura,

**Translation:** Dear Sir / Madam,

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għażiż | dear | Masculine singular form |
| Sinjur | sir; Mr. | Masculine noun |
| / | or | Indicates a choice between two |
| Sinjura | madam; Mrs. | Feminine noun |

### 1.

**Malti:** Qed nikteb biex napplika għall-impjieg ta' receptionist.

**Translation:** I am writing to apply for the position of receptionist.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Qed | am (progressive marker) | Used with a verb to indicate an ongoing action |
| nikteb | I write | First-person singular present |
| biex | in order to | Introduces a purpose clause |
| napplika | I apply | First-person singular form, loanword from apply |
| għall-impjieg | for the job | Contracted `għal` + `l-impjieg` |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| receptionist | receptionist | Loanword from receptionist |

### 2.

**Malti:** Għandi esperjenza ta' tliet snin fis-servizz tal-klijenti.

**Translation:** I have three years of experience in customer service.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għandi | I have | First-person singular form of `għand` |
| esperjenza | experience | Feminine noun |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| tliet | three | Numeral followed by a plural noun |
| snin | years | Plural of `sena` |
| fis-servizz | in service | Contracted `f'` + `is-servizz` |
| tal-klijenti | of the customers | Contracted `ta'` + `il-klijenti` (plural) |

### 3.

**Malti:** Naf nitkellem Malti, Ingliż u ftit Taljan.

**Translation:** I can speak Maltese, English, and a little Italian.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| nitkellem | I speak | First-person singular form |
| Malti | Maltese | Language name |
| Ingliż | English | Language name |
| u | and | Conjunction |

| Taljan | Italian | Language name |

### 4.

**Translation:** I can answer the phone and greet customers politely.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nista' | I can | First-person singular form |

| it-telefon | the telephone | Masculine noun with the definite article |
| u | and | Conjunction |

### 6.

**Malti:** Nista' naħdem billejl u fi tmiem il-ġimgħa.

**Translation:** I can work at night and at weekends.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nista' | I can | First-person singular form |
| naħdem | I work | First-person singular present |
| billejl | at night | Contracted form of `bil-lejl` "in the evening/night" |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| fi | in | Variant of the preposition `f'` |
| tmiem | end; close | `tmiem il-ġimgħa` is a fixed phrase meaning "weekend" |
| il-ġimgħa | the week | Feminine noun with the definite article |
