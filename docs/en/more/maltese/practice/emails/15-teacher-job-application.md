# Teacher Job Application

**Scenario:** The applicant writes to a school to apply for the position of teacher.

## Email Header

**Lil (To):** info@skola.com
**Mingħand (From):** [isem]@email.com
**Data (Date):** 15 ta' Ottubru 2026
**Suġġett (Subject):** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' għalliem

## Full Email

> **Lil:** info@skola.com
> **Mingħand:** [isem]@email.com
> **Data:** 15 ta' Ottubru 2026
> **Suġġett:** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' għalliem
>
> Għażiż Sinjur / Sinjura,
>
> Qed nikteb biex napplika għall-impjieg ta' għalliem.
>
> Għandi lawrja fl-edukazzjoni u esperjenza ta' sentejn fit-tagħlim.
>
> Naf ngħallem il-Malti, l-Ingliż u l-matematika.
>
> Jien paċenzjuż u nħobb naħdem mat-tfal.
>
> Nipprepara l-lezzjonijiet b'mod kreattiv u ċar.
>
> Jimpurtani mill-progress ta' kull student.
>
> Nista' naħdem ukoll ma' adulti fi klassijiet ta' filgħaxija.
>
> Nispera li nisma' mingħandkom dalwaqt.
>
> Grazzi talli qiesu l-applikazzjoni tiegħi.
>
> Tislijiet,
> [Ismek]

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

**Malti:** Lil: info@skola.com

**Translation:** To: info@skola.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lil | to; recipient | Maltese label for the email "To" field |
| info@skola.com | email address | The school's recruitment email (example) |

**Malti:** Mingħand: [isem]@email.com

**Translation:** From: [your name]@email.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mingħand | from; sender | Maltese label for the email "From" field; literally "from ..." |
| [isem]@email.com | email address | Placeholder for your email; replace with your real one |

**Malti:** Data: 15 ta' Ottubru 2026

**Translation:** Date: 15 October 2026

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Data | date | Email "Date" field; Maltese date format is "day ta' month year" |
| 15 | fifteen | Day in the date |
| ta' | of | Links the day and the month |
| Ottubru | October | Month name |

**Malti:** Suġġett: Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' għalliem

**Translation:** Subject: Application for the Position of Teacher

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Suġġett | subject | Email "Subject" field |
| Applikazzjoni | application | Feminine loanword from application |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| xogħol | work; job | Masculine noun |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| għalliem | teacher | Masculine noun; feminine form is `għalliema` |

**Malti:** Għażiż Sinjur / Sinjura,

**Translation:** Dear Sir / Madam,

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għażiż | dear | Masculine singular form |
| Sinjur | sir; Mr. | Masculine noun |
| / | or | Indicates a choice between two |
| Sinjura | madam; Mrs. | Feminine noun |

### 1.

**Malti:** Qed nikteb biex napplika għall-impjieg ta' għalliem.

**Translation:** I am writing to apply for the position of teacher.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Qed | am (progressive marker) | Used with a verb to indicate an ongoing action |
| nikteb | I write | First-person singular present |
| biex | in order to | Introduces a purpose clause |
| napplika | I apply | First-person singular form, loanword from apply |
| għall-impjieg | for the job | Contracted `għal` + `l-impjieg` |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| għalliem | teacher | Masculine noun |

### 2.

**Malti:** Għandi lawrja fl-edukazzjoni u esperjenza ta' sentejn fit-tagħlim.

**Translation:** I have an education degree and two years of teaching experience.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Għandi | I have | First-person singular form of `għand` |
| lawrja | degree | Feminine loanword from laurea |
| fl-edukazzjoni | in education | Contracted `f'` + `l-edukazzjoni` |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| esperjenza | experience | Feminine noun |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| sentejn | two years | Fixed form meaning "two years" |
| fit-tagħlim | in teaching | Contracted `f'` + `it-tagħlim` |

### 3.

**Malti:** Naf ngħallem il-Malti, l-Ingliż u l-matematika.

**Translation:** I can teach Maltese, English, and mathematics.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Naf | I know; I can | First-person singular form |
| ngħallem | I teach | First-person singular form |
| il-Malti | Maltese | Language name with the definite article |
| l-Ingliż | English | Language name with the definite article |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| l-matematika | mathematics | Feminine noun with the definite article |

### 4.

**Malti:** Jien paċenzjuż u nħobb naħdem mat-tfal.

**Translation:** I am patient and I like working with children.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jien | I | First-person singular pronoun |
| paċenzjuż | patient | Adjective |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| nħobb | I like | First-person singular form |
| naħdem | I work | First-person singular present |
| mat-tfal | with the children | Contracted `ma'` + `it-tfal` |

### 5.

**Malti:** Nipprepara l-lezzjonijiet b'mod kreattiv u ċar.

**Translation:** I prepare the lessons in a creative and clear way.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nipprepara | I prepare | First-person singular form |
| l-lezzjonijiet | the lessons | Plural of `lezzjoni`, with the definite article |
| b'mod | in a ... way | `b'` + `mod` "manner" |
| kreattiv | creative | Adjective loanword from creative |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| ċar | clear | Adjective |

### 6.

**Malti:** Jimpurtani mill-progress ta' kull student.

**Translation:** I care about the progress of every student.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jimpurtani | I care | `jimpurta` + `-ni` "me"; idiomatic expression |
| mill-progress | about the progress | Contracted `minn` + `il-progress` |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| kull | every; each | Determiner |
| student | student | Masculine loanword from student |

### 7.

**Malti:** Nista' naħdem ukoll ma' adulti fi klassijiet ta' filgħaxija.

**Translation:** I can also work with adults in evening classes.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nista' | I can | First-person singular form |
| naħdem | I work | First-person singular present |
| ukoll | also | Adverb |
| ma' | with | Preposition |
| adulti | adults | Plural of `adult`, loanword from adult |
| fi | in | Variant of the preposition `f'` |
| klassijiet | classes | Plural of `klassi` "class" |
| ta' | of; during | Links the noun and time expression |
| filgħaxija | in the evening | Time expression |

### 8.

**Malti:** Nispera li nisma' mingħandkom dalwaqt.

**Translation:** I hope to hear from you soon.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nispera | I hope | First-person singular form |
| li | that; introduces a clause | Conjunction |
| nisma' | I hear; receive news | First-person singular form |
| mingħandkom | from you (plural) | `mingħand` + `-kom` "you" |

### 9.

**Malti:** Grazzi talli qiesu l-applikazzjoni tiegħi.

**Translation:** Thank you for considering my application.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Grazzi | thank you | Polite phrase |
| talli | for (your) ... | Written expression introducing a reason |
| qiesu | they considered | Third-person plural past of `qies` |
| l-applikazzjoni | the application | Feminine noun with the definite article |
| tiegħi | my; mine | Possessive pronoun |

**Malti:** Tislijiet, [Ismek]

**Translation:** Regards, [your name] (formal email closing).

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tislijiet | regards; best wishes | Formal email/letter closing, literally "greetings/regards" |
| Ismek | your name | `isem` "name" + `-ek` "your"; replace with your real name |
