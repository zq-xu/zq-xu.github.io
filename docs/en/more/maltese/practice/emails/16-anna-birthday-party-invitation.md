# Birthday Party Invitation (Textbook Listening Exercise)

**Scenario:** Anna writes an email to Maria to invite her to her 15th birthday party at Għadira Bay. This is the original textbook email from the PDF, including the listening gap-fill options in brackets.

## Email Header

**Lil (To):** maria.b@gmail.com
**Mingħand (From):** anna.c@gmail.com
**Data (Date):** 30 ta' Ġunju, 2023
**Suġġett (Subject):** Festin ta' għeluq snini

## Full Email

> **Lil:** maria.b@gmail.com
> **Mingħand:** anna.c@gmail.com
> **Data:** 30 ta' Ġunju, 2023
> **Suġġett:** Festin ta' għeluq snini
>
> Ħelow Maria!
>
> Nispera li tinsab tajba. Bħalma taf, (ilbieraħ / dalwaqt / sena oħra) se nagħlaq 15-il sena u qed nibgħatek din l-ittra elettronika sabiex nistednek għall-festin ta' għeluq snini.
>
> Il-festin se jkun nhar (il-ġimgħa l-oħra / ix-xahar id-dieħel / il-Ġimgħa 14 ta' Lulju).
>
> Fis- (tmienja ta' flgħaxija / sitta ta' flgħodu / sitta ta' flgħaxija) se niltaqgħu (quddiem / fuq / taħt) il-knisja biex minn hemm niġru bil-van kollha f'daqqa li se joħodna l-bajja tal-Għadira.
>
> Fil-festin se jkun hemm ħafna ikel, xorb, logħob u mużika! Tinsiex tiġi bil-malja u ġġib xugaman għax xi qabża l-baħar ma tonqosx!
>
> Jekk jogħġbok agħtini risposta sa (bħal-lum ġimgħa / xahar ieħor).
>
> Nispera li narak ħalli nieħdu gost flimkien!
>
> Ċaw!
> Anna

## Sentence-by-Sentence Translation and Word Notes

**Malti:** Lil: maria.b@gmail.com

**Translation:** To: maria.b@gmail.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lil | to; recipient | Maltese label for the email "To" field |
| maria.b@gmail.com | email address | The recipient's address |

**Malti:** Mingħand: anna.c@gmail.com

**Translation:** From: anna.c@gmail.com

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mingħand | from; sender | Maltese label for the email "From" field; literally "from ..." |
| anna.c@gmail.com | email address | The sender's address |

**Malti:** Data: 30 ta' Ġunju, 2023

**Translation:** Date: 30 June 2023

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Data | date | Email "Date" field; Maltese date format is "day ta' month year" |
| 30 | thirty | Day in the date |
| ta' | of | Links the day and the month |
| Ġunju | June | Month name |

**Malti:** Suġġett: Festin ta' għeluq snini

**Translation:** Subject: My Birthday Celebration

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Suġġett | subject | Email "Subject" field; printed `Suggett` in the source |
| Festin | celebration; little party | Diminutive loanword |
| ta' | of | Possession linker |
| għeluq | birthday | Literally "closing"; `għeluq snin` = birthday |
| snini | my years; my birthday | `snin` (years) + `-i` (my) |

### 1.

**Malti:** Ħelow Maria!

**Translation:** Hello Maria!

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ħelow | hello | Informal greeting, loanword |
| Maria | Maria | Name |

### 2.

**Malti:** Nispera li tinsab tajba.

**Translation:** I hope you are well.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nispera | I hope | First-person singular |
| li | that | Conjunction |
| tinsab | you are | Second-person form of `tinsab` "to be located / to feel" |
| tajba | well; good | Feminine singular adjective |

### 3.

**Malti:** Bħalma taf, (ilbieraħ / dalwaqt / sena oħra) se nagħlaq 15-il sena u qed nibgħatek din l-ittra elettronika sabiex nistednek għall-festin ta' għeluq snini.

**Translation:** As you know, (yesterday / soon / another year) I am turning 15, and I am sending you this email to invite you to my birthday party.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bħalma | as; just as | `bħal` + `ma` |
| taf | you know | Second-person singular |
| ilbieraħ | yesterday | Listening option 1 |
| dalwaqt | soon | Listening option 2 |
| sena oħra | another year | Listening option 3 |
| se | will | Future marker |
| nagħlaq | I turn (age) | Literally "I close"; reaching an age |
| 15-il sena | 15 years old | Numeral + `-il` + `sena` |
| qed | am (progressive) | Progressive marker |
| nibgħatek | I send you | `nibgħat` + `-ek` (you) |
| din | this | Feminine demonstrative |
| l-ittra elettronika | the email | `ittra` (letter) + `elettronika` (electronic) |
| sabiex | in order to | Formal conjunction |
| nistednek | I invite you | `nistedin` + `-ek` |
| għall-festin | to the party | Contracted `għal` + `il-festin` |
| ta' għeluq snini | of my birthday | `għeluq` (birthday) + `snin` (years) + `-i` (my) |

### 4.

**Malti:** Il-festin se jkun nhar (il-ġimgħa l-oħra / ix-xahar id-dieħel / il-Ġimgħa 14 ta' Lulju).

**Translation:** The party will be on (next week / next month / Friday 14 July).

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Il-festin | the party | `festin` (little party), loanword |
| se jkun | will be | Future construction |
| nhar | on the day of | Literally "day" |
| il-ġimgħa l-oħra | the other week; next week | Listening option 1 |
| ix-xahar id-dieħel | the coming month; next month | Listening option 2 |
| il-Ġimgħa 14 ta' Lulju | Friday 14 July | Listening option 3 |

### 5.

**Malti:** Fis- (tmienja ta' flgħaxija / sitta ta' flgħodu / sitta ta' flgħaxija) se niltaqgħu (quddiem / fuq / taħt) il-knisja biex minn hemm niġru bil-van kollha f'daqqa li se joħodna l-bajja tal-Għadira.

**Translation:** At (8 pm / 6 am / 6 pm) we will meet (in front of / above / below) the church, and from there we will all drive together by van to Għadira Bay.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| tmienja ta' flgħaxija | eight in the evening | Listening option 1 (`filgħaxija`) |
| sitta ta' flgħodu | six in the morning | Listening option 2 (`filgħodu`) |
| sitta ta' flgħaxija | six in the evening | Listening option 3 |
| quddiem | in front of | Listening option 1 (position) |
| fuq | above; on top | Listening option 2 |
| taħt | below; under | Listening option 3 |
| il-knisja | the church | Feminine noun with the definite article |
| biex | in order to | Purpose conjunction |
| minn hemm | from there | Place phrase |
| niġru | we drive quickly | First-person plural of `iġri` "to run / hurry" |
| bil-van | by van | Contracted `b'` + `il-van` |
| kollha f'daqqa | all together | `kollha` (all) + `f'daqqa` (at once) |
| se joħodna | will take us | `jieħu` (take) + `-na` (us) |
| l-bajja tal-Għadira | Għadira Bay | `bajja` (bay) + `tal-` (of) + `Għadira` (place name) |

### 6.

**Malti:** Fil-festin se jkun hemm ħafna ikel, xorb, logħob u mużika!

**Translation:** At the party there will be lots of food, drink, games and music!

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fil-festin | at the party | Contracted `f'` + `il-festin` |
| se jkun hemm | there will be | Existential construction |
| ħafna | lots of | Quantifier |
| ikel | food | Masculine noun |
| xorb | drink | Noun |
| logħob | games | Noun |
| mużika | music | Loanword |

### 7.

**Malti:** Tinsiex tiġi bil-malja u ġġib xugaman għax xi qabża l-baħar ma tonqosx!

**Translation:** Don't forget to come in your swimsuit and bring a towel, because a dip in the sea is a must!

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tinsiex | don't forget | `tinsa` + `x`, negative imperative |
| tiġi | you come | Second-person singular |
| bil-malja | in a swimsuit | Contracted `b'` + `il-malja` (swimsuit) |
| u | and | Conjunction |
| ġġib | you bring | Second-person singular |
| xugaman | towel | Loanword |
| għax | because | Causal conjunction |
| xi | a; some | Determiner |
| qabża l-baħar | a jump in the sea | `qabża` (jump) + `l-baħar` (sea) |
| ma tonqosx | won't be missing | `ma` + `tonqos` + `x` negation; idiom "a must" |

### 8.

**Malti:** Jekk jogħġbok agħtini risposta sa (bħal-lum ġimgħa / xahar ieħor).

**Translation:** Please give me a reply within (a week from today / a month's time).

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jekk jogħġbok | please | Literally "if it pleases you" |
| agħtini | give me | `agħti` (give) + `-ni` (me) |
| risposta | reply; answer | Loanword |
| sa | by; until | Preposition |
| bħal-lum ġimgħa | a week from today | Listening option 1 (one OCR option is unclear, possibly `il-biċċa l-oħra`) |
| xahar ieħor | another month; a month later | Listening option 2 |

### 9.

**Malti:** Nispera li narak ħalli nieħdu gost flimkien!

**Translation:** I hope to see you so that we can have fun together!

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nispera | I hope | First-person singular |
| li | that | Conjunction |
| narak | I see you | `nara` + `-k` (you) |
| ħalli | so that | Colloquial conjunction |
| nieħdu gost | we have fun | `ieħu gost` idiom "to enjoy" |
| flimkien | together | Adverb |

### 10.

**Malti:** Ċaw!

**Translation:** Bye!

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ċaw | bye | Informal farewell |

## Formal vs Informal Openings and Closings (from the same PDF page)

| Item | Formal (Formali) | Informal (Informali) |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | Għażiż/a Sur ..., Sinjura ... (Dear Mr ... / Mrs ...) | Ħelow, Ħaw', Bongu... (Hello, Hi, Good morning...) |
| Closing | Saħħa, Tislijiet, Dejjem tiegħek (Take care, Regards, Yours truly) | Ċaw, Sellili, Narak! (Bye, say hello for me, See you!) |
