# Chef Job Application (Textbook Writing Template)

**Scenario:** An applicant writes to a restaurant to apply for the position of chef. This is the original textbook template from the PDF, including the gap-fill options in brackets. Note: the closing is printed `Tisjiet` in the source; the standard formal spelling is `Tislijiet`.

## Email Header

**Lil (To):** info@restorant.com
**Mingħand (From):** gorg.com.mt
**Data (Date):** 16 ta' Ottubru 2026
**Suġġett (Subject):** Applikazzjoni ta' xogħol ta' kok

### 2.

**Malti:** Jien qiegħed nibgħatlek din l-ittra biex (napplika / tapplika) għal xogħol.

**Translation:** I am sending you this letter to (apply / you apply) for a job.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jien | I | First-person pronoun |
| qiegħed | am (progressive) | Progressive marker, masculine |
| nibgħatlek | I send you | `nibgħat` + `-lek` (to you) |
| din l-ittra | this letter | `din` (this) + `ittra` (letter) |
| biex | in order to | Purpose conjunction |
| napplika | I apply | Gap-fill option 1: loanword `apply`, first person |
| tapplika | you apply | Gap-fill option 2: second person |
| għal xogħol | for a job | `għal` (for) + `xogħol` (job) |

### 3.

**Malti:** Nispera li tinsab tajjeb. Qed nibgħatlek din l-ittra elettronika sabiex napplika għal xogħol ta' kok fir-restorant tiegħek.

**Translation:** I hope you are well. I am sending you this email to apply for the position of chef in your restaurant.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nispera | I hope | First-person singular |
| li | that | Conjunction |
| tinsab | you are | Second-person form of `tinsab` |
| tajjeb | well | Adverb/adjective |
| Qed | am (progressive) | Progressive marker |
| nibgħatlek | I send you | See above |
| din l-ittra elettronika | this email | `ittra` + `elettronika` |
| sabiex | in order to | Formal conjunction |
| napplika | I apply | First-person singular |
| għal xogħol ta' kok | for a chef's job | `kok` (chef) |
| fir-restorant | in the restaurant | Contracted `f'` + `ir-restorant` |
| tiegħek | your | Masculine possessive |

### 4.

**Translation:** I want to work as a chef because I love cooking, I love working with others, and I love meeting people.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jien | I | Pronoun |
| nixtieq | I want | First-person singular |
| naħdem | I work | First-person singular |
| bħala | as | Preposition |
| kok | chef | Loanword |
| għax | because | Causal conjunction |
| inħobb | I love | First-person singular |
| insajjar | I cook | First-person singular |
| ma' ħaddieħor | with others | `ħaddieħor` (someone else / others) |

| niltaqa' | I meet | First-person singular |

### 5.

**Malti:** Kont immur l-iskola tal-ITS (institute of tourism studies) u minn hemm ġibt iċ-ċertifikati li kelli bżonn.

**Translation:** I used to attend the ITS school (Institute of Tourism Studies) and from there I got the certificates I needed.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Kont | I used to | Past auxiliary |
| immur | I go | First-person singular |
| l-iskola | the school | Feminine noun with the definite article |
| tal-ITS | of the ITS | Contracted `ta'` + `l-ITS` |
| minn hemm | from there | Place phrase |
| ġibt | I got; I brought | First-person singular past |
| iċ-ċertifikati | the certificates | Plural of `ċertifikat` with the definite article |
| li | that; which | Relative pronoun |
| kelli bżonn | I needed | `kell` (had) + `bżonn` (need) |

### 8.

**Malti:** Grazzi talli qrajt din l-ittra. Napprezza jekk tikkunsidrani għal dan ix-xogħol.

**Translation:** Thank you for reading this letter. I would appreciate it if you considered me for this job.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Grazzi | thank you | Polite expression |
| talli | for (your) ... | Written expression introducing a reason |
| qrajt | you read | Second-person singular past of `qara` |
| din l-ittra | this letter | See above |
| Napprezza | I appreciate | Loanword |
| jekk | if | Conditional conjunction |
| tikkunsidrani | you consider me | `tikkunsidra` + `-ni` (me) |
| għal dan ix-xogħol | for this job | See above |

### 9.

**Malti:** Nista' niltaqa' miegħek biex nikkellmu iktar fid-dettall kuljum mit-8 ta' filgħodu sas-7 ta' filgħaxija. / meta tkun komdu inti.

**Translation:** I can meet you to talk in more detail any day from 8 in the morning until 7 in the evening. / Or whenever you are comfortable.

| Maltese | English meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nista' | I can | First-person singular |
| niltaqa' | I meet | First-person singular |
| miegħek | with you | `ma'` + `-ek` (you) |
| biex | in order to | Purpose conjunction |
| nikkellmu | we talk | First-person plural of `kellem` |
| iktar | more | Comparative adverb |
| fid-dettall | in detail | Contracted `f'` + `id-dettall` |
| kuljum | daily; every day | Adverb |
| mit-8 ta' filgħodu | from 8 in the morning | Contracted `minn` + `it-8` |
| sas-7 ta' filgħaxija | until 7 in the evening | Contracted `sa` + `is-7` |
| meta | when | Time conjunction |
| tkun komdu | you are comfortable | `tkun` (are) + `komdu` (comfortable) |
| inti | you | Second-person pronoun |
