Key Review Points ​

  1. Shopping politeness phrases: agħtini (give me), jekk jogħġbok (please), x’għandi nagħtik? (how much should I pay you?), bqija (change).
  2. Ingredient expressions: gramma (gram), millilitru (millilitre), kuċċarina (teaspoon), nofs (half), ftit (a little).
  3. Common recipe imperatives: itfa’ (put in), ħawwad (stir), żid (add), farrak (crush), ifrex (spread), aħmi (bake).
  4. Dressing agreement: liebes (m), liebsa (f), libsin / lebsin (plural); colours agree with the noun they modify, not mechanically with the wearer's gender.
  5. Number structure: erbgħa standalone "four", erba’ snin "four years"; tliet kotba three books, ħdax-il ktieb eleven books, għoxrin ktieb twenty books.
  6. Learn the dual as whole words: jumejn two days, sagħtejn two hours, ġimagħtejn two weeks, xahrejn two months, sentejn two years, darbtejn twice.
